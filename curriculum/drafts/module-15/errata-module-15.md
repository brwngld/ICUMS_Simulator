## CHANGES MADE

1. lesson-15.4.md — modal-shift worked example, three strings:
   - "takes around five weeks port to port" → "takes around three weeks port
     to port" (Step 3);
   - "goods at sea for around five weeks are pipeline stock" → "...around three
     weeks..." (Step 4);
   - FACTS bullet "one week air and five weeks sea transit" → "...three
     weeks...".
   Reason: although labelled illustrative, the figure was implausible enough to
   mislead. Verified by WebSearch: Tema–Rotterdam direct sea is roughly 10–14
   days port to port, with transshipment roughly 18–25 days; five weeks occurs
   only on slow indirect services. Three weeks is a plausible transshipment
   figure and preserves the pedagogy (pipeline stock, segmentation logic,
   inventory-consequence check). No other lesson content depends on the number.
