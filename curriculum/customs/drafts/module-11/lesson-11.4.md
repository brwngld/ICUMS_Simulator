=== KEY CONCEPT ===
HEADING: The three crossings, mapped
BODY:
The free zone's goods move in three directions, and each crossing has its own customs treatment: zone to zone (goods moving between designated places, staying within the zone regime); zone to foreign market (the goods exported, leaving Ghana's territory without home-use duty); and zone to the customs territory (the goods entering free circulation, the home-use machinery running).

The crossings' provisions are the movements' provisions: section 94's free-zone frame, sections 95 and 96's controlled movements, section 97's exports. The learner's map: for each crossing, name the movement (into the zone, out to the market, into the territory), name the provisions that carry it, and name the duty treatment the movement produces.

The map's discipline: the crossings are the goods' destiny's branches — and the branch picked is the duty's decision. The goods that export pay no home-use duty; the goods that enter the territory pay the home-use machinery's; the goods that move zone to zone stay in the deferral.

=== KEY CONCEPT ===
HEADING: Zone to zone: the goods' move between designated places
BODY:
The goods' move between free zones stays within the zone regime: the goods leave one designated place and enter another, the deferral continuing across the move. The movement's provisions are the controlled-movement machinery — the goods under customs control for the move, the identification travelling with them, the records accounting at both ends.

The learner's discipline: the zone-to-zone move is the suspense regime's removal at zone scale — the goods' deferral travelling with them, the movement's provisions naming the control. The records' discipline (section 9; the zone's records) accounts for the goods at both ends, and the movement's proof is the goods' identity through the transfer.

The practice's care: the zone-to-zone move's documentation names both zones, the goods, and the deferral's continuation — because the movement that looks like an import (goods leaving one place for another) is legally the deferral's continuation, and the file must show it.

=== KEY CONCEPT ===
HEADING: Zone to the foreign market: the export crossing
BODY:
The goods' exit from the zone to a foreign market is the export crossing: section 97's provisions frame it — goods placed on board for consignment outside the country (the vessel branch), the declaration passed and the goods crossing the boundary (the overland branch). The zone's goods leaving for the foreign market are exports — and no Ghanaian home-use duty attaches, because the goods never entered free circulation.

The crossing's evidence: the export declaration, the transport document, the goods' identification through the exit — the export machinery's file. The learner's discipline: the zone's export is the export machinery's movement, and the zone's regime carried the goods to it without the duty — the deferral's closure by departure.

The commercial value: the zone's manufacturer's exports are the zone regime's product — the inputs entered without home-use duty, manufactured in the zone, exported without it. The duty's question was deferred by the zone and answered by the export: never paid, because never due.

=== KEY CONCEPT ===
HEADING: Zone to the customs territory: the home-use crossing
BODY:
The goods' exit from the zone into Ghana's free circulation is the home-use crossing: the goods' destiny changed to the Ghanaian market, and the home-use machinery runs — the declaration under section 49, the duty assessed on the customs value, the controls under section 3, the release under section 6(1).

The crossing's duty question: the goods' value and classification are determined as for any home-use entry — the zone's deferral ends, and the duty is charged as if the goods were entering from abroad, because free circulation is what the entry achieves. The origin question may travel with the goods (Module 9's machinery): the zone's processing's effect on the goods' origin is the origin rules' question.

The learner's discipline: the zone-to-territory crossing is the home-use machinery's entry — the deferral's end by payment — and the file's trail is the home-use clearance's: declaration, value, classification, duty, controls, release.
