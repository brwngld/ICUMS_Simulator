=== ASSESSMENT Q1 | tests lesson 12.1 ===
QUESTION:
Which option best states section 3(2)'s two objectives for the risk-based control, in the provision's own words?

POINTS: 1
OPTIONS:
1. Increasing the examinations' number and reducing the clearance times.
2. Identifying and evaluating risks, and developing counter-measures on the basis of risk-management criteria developed and updated regularly from international, national and local level data.
3. Eliminating all risk from the international trade and securing every consignment.
4. Matching the examination's intensity to the trader's payment history.
CORRECT: 2
EXPLANATION:
The correct answer is Answer 2. Section 3(2) of Act 891 states the objectives in its own words: a customs control shall primarily be based on risk management, with the objective of identifying and evaluating risks, and developing counter-measures on the basis of risk management criteria developed and updated regularly from international, national and local level data. The two objectives are the module's definition of done — the risks identified and evaluated, the counter-measures built on the data's three levels and regularly updated.

Answer 1 substitutes revenue and staffing for the provision's objectives — the revenue's protection is the objectives' consequence, not their statement. Answer 3 is the uniform-examination design the risk-based method replaces — the provision's method is the selection, not the totality. Answer 4's elimination is not the objective — the risks are managed, not eliminated; the uncertainty's effect is what the criteria measure.

The takeaway: the objectives are the provision's own words — identify and evaluate, counter-measure on the data's levels — and the module's machinery is their implementation.

OPTIONS:

3. The criteria are disclosed to the trader's licensed agent on written request.
4. The criteria are disclosed if the trader pays the administration's costs.
CORRECT: 2
EXPLANATION:
The correct answer is Answer 2. Section 3(4) of Act 891 provides that specific information regarding risk management — including the risk register, the risk profile and other risk assessment data — is confidential. The trader's demand for the criteria is answered by the provision's protection: the criteria are the confidential risk data. The trader's protections are the clearance's conditions and the dispute machinery: the release's conditions under section 6(1) and the objection under section 13.

Answer 1 invents a post-examination disclosure that the confidentiality denies — the criteria's protection does not end with the examination. Answer 3 invents an agent-disclosure route the provision does not carry — the agents hold the same protection. Answer 4 invents a payment-based disclosure; the criteria's confidentiality is not a fee's subject.

The takeaway: the criteria's confidentiality is statutory — the trader's protections run through the clearance's conditions and the dispute machinery, and the criteria stay behind section 3(4)'s wall.
