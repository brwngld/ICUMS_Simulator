=== KEY CONCEPT ===
HEADING: The profile versus the register
BODY:
The risk register records the risks; the risk profile operationalises them. The register's entry states the risk's anatomy — the cause, the event, the impact, the analysis, the treatment. The profile is the targeting instrument built from the entries: the criteria that decide, before a consignment arrives, which consignments deserve a look.

The deck's control levels frame the profile's place: pre-arrival (the advance data reviewed), clearance (the selected goods and documents checked), post-clearance (the declarations and records verified), investigation (the suspected offences pursued) — and the intelligence supports the decisions across all four levels. The profile is the pre-arrival and clearance levels' selection instrument: the criteria the system applies to the declarations as they arrive.

The learner's distinction: the register is the risk's record; the profile is the risk's application. The register's entry can exist without a profile; the profile is built from the entries' evaluated risks. The two instruments' life is the cycle's: the register's entries are the cycle's product, and the profiles are the cycle's application — the record feeding the instrument, the instrument applying the record's risks to the arriving consignments.

=== KEY CONCEPT ===
HEADING: The indicator types: what profiles read
BODY:
The profiles' indicators are the patterns the criteria read, and the lesson's categories are the practice's map — the deck's clearance and pre-arrival slides' signals.

The trader's behaviour: the declarations' history, the amendments' pattern, the compliance record. The goods: the categories, the classifications' sensitivity, the restriction's presence. The origin and the route: the supply chain's geography, the transhipment's pattern, the corridor's risk. The value patterns: the prices against the market's, the valuation's history.

The categories are the profile's read: a consignment's data points are read against the indicators' patterns, and the match's weight is the selection's basis. The categories are the deck's and the practice's — and the specific indicators' values are the administration's, confidential under section 3(4). The learner's read of any profile is at the categories' level: the goods' sensitivity, the trader's history, the route's geography — the categories name the pattern's kind, and the values are the administration's confidential data.

=== KEY CONCEPT ===
HEADING: Intelligence: the sources and the sharing

The profiles' criteria are fed by intelligence, and the sources' frame is the deck's and the provision's.

Section 3(3) provides the international sharing: the Authority, in conjunction with cooperating foreign customs administrations, may carry out joint control and targeting activities based on risk management — the cross-border intelligence's statutory basis. Section 3(2)(b) provides the data's levels: the international, the national and the local — the intelligence's geography.

The deck's intelligence slide states its role: the intelligence supports the decisions across all four control levels — the pre-arrival's reviews, the clearance's checks, the post-clearance's audits, the investigations' pursuits. The learner's reading: the intelligence is the criteria's feed, and the confidentiality (section 3(4)) is the feed's protection — the intelligence's specifics are confidential, and the sharing's authority is the provision's.

=== KEY CONCEPT ===
HEADING: Targeting: the selection's rule
BODY:
The targeting is the profile's application to the declarations: the criteria applied, the selections made, the treatments assigned. The selection's rule is section 3's: the controls are primarily risk-based (section 3(2)) and include random checks (section 3(1)) — the targeting is the risk's application, and the randomness is the design's protection against the known pattern's gaming.

The learner's reading of a selection: the selection's basis is the profile's criteria (confidential), applied to the consignment's data (the declaration's particulars, the trader's history, the route's pattern). The selection's lawfulness is the provision's method — and the trader's challenge is the confidentiality's boundary, answered by section 3(4).

The discipline: the targeting's rule is the provisions' — the risk's primacy and the randomness's inclusion — and the learner's descriptions of the targeting state the rule, never the criteria.

=== KEY CONCEPT ===
HEADING: The confidentiality of the profiles
BODY:
Section 3(4)'s confidentiality is the profiles' operating condition, and the lesson's discipline is the profile's handling.

The protected subjects: the risk register, the risk profile, and the other risk assessment data. The protected operations: the profiles' criteria are not disclosed to the profiled traders; the profiles' drafts are handled within the protection; the profiles' effectiveness data is the administration's.

The learner's practice: the profile's work is the administration's work, and the professional's advice does not carry the criteria. The trader's question — "why was I targeted?" — is answered by the provision's confidentiality, and the trader's remedies are the clearance's conditions and the dispute machinery. The confidentiality's boundary is the trader's and the professional's — and the profiles' work runs inside it. The protection's purpose is the targeting's integrity: the criteria the traders cannot see are the criteria the traders cannot game — and the confidential profile is the fair profile, applied to every consignment by the same unseen rule.

=== KEY CONCEPT ===
HEADING: The profile's lifecycle: built, tested, retired
BODY:
The profiles are artifacts with lives, and the deck's process cycle frames them: the profile is built from the register's evaluated risks (the context's criteria applied); tested against the declarations' outcomes (the selections' findings); and retired or updated when the criteria's data moves (the review's loop).

The lifecycle's discipline: the profile's criteria are the criteria's data's — the data updates, the criteria update; the criteria's "developed and updated regularly" (section 3(2)(b)) is the profile's lifecycle's statutory phrase. The profile that never updates is the criteria that never update — the provision's phrase left unperformed.

The learner's practice: the profiles' lifecycle is the administration's, and the learner's understanding of the lifecycle is the profile's reading — a profile's criteria are the data's current expression, not the data's permanent truth.

=== MCQ 1 ===
QUESTION:
A trainee asks how a risk profile differs from a risk register. Which option best states the distinction?

POINTS: 1
OPTIONS:
1. The register is confidential; the profile is published to traders.
2. The register records the risks' anatomy (the cause, the event, the impact, the analysis, the treatment); the profile is the targeting instrument built from the register's evaluated risks — the criteria that decide, before a consignment arrives, which consignments deserve a look.
3. The register is the traders' document; the profile is the officers'.
4. There is no difference; the terms are interchangeable.
CORRECT: 2
EXPLANATION:
The correct answer is Answer 2. The lesson's distinction block states the instruments' difference: the register records the risks' anatomy — the cause, the event, the impact, the analysis, the treatment, the review — per the deck's risk definition and the cycle's method. The profile is the targeting instrument built from the register's evaluated risks — the criteria applied to the arriving consignments, the selections made before the goods' clearance.

Answer 1 inverts the confidentiality: section 3(4) protects both the register and the profile — the risk data is confidential, and neither is published. Answer 3 misassigns the ownership: both are the administration's instruments. Answer 4 abandons the distinction the module's structure teaches — the register is the record, the profile is the application.

The takeaway: the register records the risk; the profile applies it — the record's anatomy and the application's criteria are different instruments, both confidential, both built from the same cycle.

The Authority plans a joint targeting operation with a neighbouring administration's customs service. Which option best states the provision that authorises the cooperation, and its basis?
