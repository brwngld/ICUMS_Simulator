=== KEY CONCEPT ===
HEADING: The risk cycle: the method's order
BODY:
The risk deck's process slide states the cycle's order, and the learner holds it as the method: establish the context (the objectives and the criteria); identify the risks; analyse the likelihood and the consequence; evaluate and prioritise; treat the risks (select and apply the controls); and monitor and review — with documentation, communication and consultation supporting every stage.

The cycle's order is the method's discipline: the context is set before the risks are identified; the risks are identified before they are analysed; the analysis feeds the evaluation; the evaluation's priorities drive the treatment; and the treatment's outcomes are monitored and reviewed — feeding the context again. The cycle is the objectives' implementation: section 3(2)'s identifying and evaluating, and the counter-measures' development, are the cycle's steps in the Act's words. The supporting activities — the documentation, the communication, the consultation — run through every stage, because the cycle's product is the written record the next cycle reads.

The learner's discipline: the risk work runs in the cycle's order — a register entry built before the context is set, or a treatment applied before the evaluation, is the method's breach before it is a work flaw.

=== KEY CONCEPT ===
HEADING: The register's anatomy: what an entry carries
BODY:
The risk register is the cycle's written product, and its anatomy follows the deck's method's parts.

The risk's statement: the cause (the weakness or source of uncertainty), the event (the possible breach or harmful occurrence), and the impact (the effect on customs objectives) — the deck's risk definition's three parts, per entry. The analysis: the likelihood and the consequence assessed. The evaluation: the risk's priority against the criteria. The treatment: the controls selected and applied. The review: the outcomes monitored and the risk updated.

The register's discipline: the entries are written, dated and owned — the documentation, communication and consultation the deck's process slide names as supporting every stage. A register entry whose cause, event and impact are stated can be analysed and treated; an entry that is only a worry cannot. The learner's practice: every risk the learner states is stated in the register's anatomy — cause, event, impact, analysis, treatment, review.

=== KEY CONCEPT ===
HEADING: The data sources: the three levels
BODY:
Section 3(2)(b) states the counter-measures' basis: risk management criteria developed and updated regularly from international, national and local level data. The three levels are the criteria's sources, and the learner holds them as the data's geography.

The international level: the conventions' and the WCO's frameworks' data — the patterns the international trade shows, the enforcement alerts the partners share. The national level: the country's trade data, the national seizure patterns, the revenue's risks. The local level: the port's and the post's own experience — the examination findings, the local traders' patterns, the terminal's specifics.

The levels' discipline: the criteria are developed and updated regularly — the provision's own phrase — and the update's inputs are the three levels' data. A criterion that never updates is a criterion the provision's phrase has left: the data's regular refresh is the criteria's life.

=== KEY CONCEPT ===
HEADING: Confidentiality: the register's protection in operation
BODY:
Section 3(4)'s confidentiality is the register's operating condition: the risk register, the risk profile and the other risk assessment data are confidential — and the register's work runs inside the protection.

The protection's operations: the register's entries are not disclosed to the traders the profiles target; the profiles' criteria are not revealed in the examinations' outcomes; the assessment data is not produced in the trader's disputes. The consultant's controlled access (section 3(5)) is the protection's designed exception: the expertise in, the confidentiality maintained.

The learner's discipline: the risk work's documents are handled within the protection — the register's copies, the profiles' drafts, the assessment's data — and the discipline extends to the professional's conversations: the trader's question about the criteria is answered by the provision, and the professional's file does not carry the criteria's copies.

=== KEY CONCEPT ===
HEADING: The context: the cycle's first step
BODY:
The deck's process slide begins the cycle with establishing the context, and the context's steps are the risk work's grounding.

State the objective at risk: revenue, security or facilitation — the objective whose harm the risk measures. Define the activity, the operating environment and the scope: where the risk lives and what the analysis covers. Assess the resources, the responsibilities and the existing controls: what the risk work has to work with. Set the risk criteria before assessing and comparing threats: the measures the evaluation will use.

The context's discipline: the criteria are set before the assessment — the evaluation's measures are chosen before the risks are compared, so the comparison's results are the criteria's results. The learner's practice: the context's statement is the risk work's first document — the objectives, the scope, the criteria — and the register's entries are assessed against the stated criteria, not against the assessor's mood.

=== WORKED EXAMPLE ===
HEADING: A risk register entry built from a seizure pattern
BODY:
Step 1. The context. The examination team's context: the objective at risk is the import revenue; the scope is the bonded warehouse's removals; the criteria are the likelihood and the consequence scales the framework sets.

Step 2. The pattern found. Three examinations in two months find the same under-declaration pattern: goods declared at a lower classification's value, the trader's invoices reworked between lodgement and examination. The pattern is the risk's signal.

Step 3. The entry built. The register's entry states the anatomy: the cause — the trader's invoice reworking; the event — the under-declared consignments' clearance; the impact — the revenue's loss on the value difference. The analysis: the likelihood (the pattern's recurrence) and the consequence (the duty's difference). The evaluation: the risk's priority against the criteria.

Step 4. The treatment selected. The treatment: the trader's next consignments examined; the invoices verified against the source documents; the pattern's finding recorded for the targeting. The treatment's basis: the criteria's data — the examinations' findings, the local level.

Step 5. The review set. The entry's review is dated: the treatment's outcomes monitored, the risk updated when the pattern changes. The confidentiality holds: the entry's contents stay in the register.

What does this example demonstrate? The register entry is the cycle's product — the context's criteria, the anatomy's parts, the treatment's selection, the review's date — and the entry's contents stay confidential.

Key lesson: the risk stated in the register's anatomy is the risk the machinery can treat — the worry without the anatomy is the method's first gap.

=== WORKED EXAMPLE ===
HEADING: A register abroad: the cycle's same order
BODY:
Step 1. The scene. A foreign administration's risk team builds a register entry for an emerging e-commerce risk: the small-parcel flow's growth outpacing the inspection capacity.

Step 2. The cycle's order. The context: the objective (the revenue and the security), the scope (the express and postal flows), the criteria. The identification: the risk's cause (the flow's growth), the event (the undeclared or misdeclared parcels' clearance), the impact (the revenue and the security's exposure). The analysis and evaluation: the likelihood and consequence assessed against the criteria. The treatment: the advance-data checks, the selectivity's adjustments.

Step 3. The review's loop. The treatment's outcomes are monitored: the parcels' compliance measured, the risk updated — the cycle's review feeding the context again.

Step 4. The transfer. The cycle's order and the register's anatomy are the method's constants; the risks' subjects are the local layer. The learner who knows the cycle reads any administration's register correctly.

What does this example demonstrate? The risk cycle's order is the international method — and the register's anatomy is its written product, in every administration that runs the method.

Key lesson: the cycle's order is portable; the risks' subjects are local — and the learner's skill is the order, applied to the local risks.

QUESTION:
Which option best states the order of the risk cycle as the training deck's process slide presents it?

POINTS: 1
OPTIONS:
1. Treat, review, identify, analyse, evaluate, context.
2. Establish the context; identify the risks; analyse the likelihood and consequence; evaluate and prioritise; treat the risks; monitor and review — with documentation, communication and consultation supporting every stage.
3. Identify, treat, review, context, analyse, evaluate — the order adapts to each risk.
4. The cycle's order is not stated; the risks are worked as they arrive.
CORRECT: 2
EXPLANATION:
The correct answer is Answer 2. The training deck's process slides state the cycle's order: establishing the context (the objectives and the criteria); identifying the risks; analysing the likelihood and the consequence; evaluating and prioritising; treating the risks (selecting and applying the controls); and monitoring and reviewing the outcomes — with the documentation, communication and consultation supporting every stage. The order is the method's discipline: each step's output feeds the next, and the review feeds the context again.

Answer 1 inverts the cycle's start — the treatment runs after the analysis and evaluation. Answer 3's adaptive order is the method's breach: the cycle's stages run in their order, and the adaptation is within the stages, not across them. Answer 4 abandons the method the deck teaches and the provision's objectives require.

The takeaway: the cycle's order is the method — context before identification, analysis before evaluation, treatment after, review throughout — and the learner's risk work runs in that order.
