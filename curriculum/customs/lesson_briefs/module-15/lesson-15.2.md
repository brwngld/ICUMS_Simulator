# LESSON BRIEF 15.2 — Client due diligence, advisory work and evidence management

Course 5: Advanced Border Controls, Compliance and Professional Practice · difficulty `advanced`
Module 15: Professional Customs Practice and Capstone · lesson 2 of 4 · module 15 of 15
Proposed slug: `client-due-diligence-advisory-work-and-evidence-management`

## Summary

The agent's first duty is to know the client and the goods before declaring them:
advice built on unverified facts becomes the agent's false statement. Act 891's
record-keeping and information provisions define what evidence the professional must
hold and produce. This lesson teaches due diligence, the advisory standard, and the
evidence discipline that protects both the client and the licence.

## Learning Objectives

State a due-diligence standard for new clients and new goods: what to verify before the first declaration.
Explain the advisory standard: advice traces to a provision or a verified document, never to "it usually works".
Describe evidence management under ss.9 and 11: what is kept, for how long, and what is produced on demand.
Connect the false-statement definition (s.151) to advisory work: the declaration is the agent's own statement.

## Key Terms

record :: any information kept in the ordinary course of business that pertains to any customs activity, in the nature of a statement, declaration, document, data, book, paper, correspondence, accounts, and financial accounting data
false statement :: a statement made with respect to goods subject to customs control that is false or misleading in a material respect or a material omission from a required statement or document with respect to goods subject to customs control
customs business :: an activity that has to do with the importation or exportation of goods
declaration :: a statutory form filed or to be filed with respect to imported or exported goods at the time of entry or export

## Source anchors

- `ACT` s.151 — the Key Terms above, verbatim from the Interpretation section.
- `ACT` s.9 — record keeping; s.11 — provision of information; s.14 — documents to be
  submitted.
- `ACT` s.10 — Customs Laboratory, as the verification resource a professional may
  reach for on doubtful goods (cited; the laboratory's functions are prescribed by
  Regulations).
