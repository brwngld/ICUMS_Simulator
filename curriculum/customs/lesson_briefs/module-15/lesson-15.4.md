# LESSON BRIEF 15.4 — End-to-end customs clearance capstone case

Course 5: Advanced Border Controls, Compliance and Professional Practice · difficulty `advanced`
Module 15: Professional Customs Practice and Capstone · lesson 4 of 4 · module 15 of 15
Proposed slug: `end-to-end-customs-clearance-capstone-case`

## Summary

One consignment, every lesson at once: a Ghanaian trader's container arrives at Tema,
and the learner takes it from the bill of lading to release — classifying, valuing,
checking origin and permits, managing the agency hold that appears, and closing the
file with the records the law requires. No new facts are taught in this lesson: every
step traces to a provision and a lesson already taught.

## Learning Objectives

Run the end-to-end case: arrival (s.16), declaration (ss.48–50), supporting documents (s.14), classification (Module 7), valuation (Module 8), origin and permits (Module 9, Module 6), release (s.6).
Handle the case's deliberate complications — a restricted-goods permit, a valuation query, a missing document (s.51) — using the correct provision for each.
Close the file correctly: records (s.9), the audit trail's shape (s.7), and what must be retrievable years later.
Demonstrate the professional standard: every decision in the case names its legal basis.

## Key Terms

declarant :: a person who makes a declaration for customs purposes, and who is the owner of the goods, the importer, exporter, or the agent
declaration :: a statutory form filed or to be filed with respect to imported or exported goods at the time of entry or export
duty :: customs duty, excise duty, tax or other charges collectible on imported or exported goods
record :: any information kept in the ordinary course of business that pertains to any customs activity, in the nature of a statement, declaration, document, data, book, paper, correspondence, accounts, and financial accounting data

## Source anchors

- Every anchor from the programme is available here; the case introduces none. The

  traces to.
- `SPECIMEN` — the Form 80 allocation letter specimen, if the case needs a document

**Fact-check caution.** The case is constructed and generic (spec C5): no real company,
no real shipment. Every figure is illustrative and labelled (C4.3); every rate carries
the C4.2 label; every legal step cites its provision. This lesson's failure mode is
re-teaching — keep each step short and point back to the lesson that owns it.
