# LESSON BRIEF 15.1 — Ethics, code of conduct and customs house agent management

Course 5: Advanced Border Controls, Compliance and Professional Practice · difficulty `advanced`
Module 15: Professional Customs Practice and Capstone · lesson 1 of 4 · module 15 of 15
Proposed slug: `ethics-code-of-conduct-and-customs-house-agent-management`

## Summary

Practice stands on ethics: the licensed agent holds other people's money, goods and
reputations, and Act 891's licensing and authority provisions are the floor of the
profession — not the ceiling. The Act defines the customs house agent and the customs
business the licence covers; the deck and the legal anchors carry the conduct
expectations. This lesson teaches the profession's ethical base and the management
duties that come with the licence.

## Learning Objectives

State what the licence to carry out customs business authorises (ss.43–44, and the Act's definitions of agent and customs business).
Explain the written-authority duty (s.44) as an ethical instrument, not just a formality.
Identify the conduct expectations a licensed agent carries — accuracy of declarations, client confidentiality, and the offences that end careers (bribes, s.139; impersonation, s.133) — cited, not priced.
Describe the agent-management duties of a firm employing agents: authority records, competence, and supervision.

## Key Terms

customs house agent :: a person who is granted a licence to carry out customs business
customs business :: an activity that has to do with the importation or exportation of goods
agent :: a person performing customs business on behalf of another person
record :: any information kept in the ordinary course of business that pertains to any customs activity, in the nature of a statement, declaration, document, data, book, paper, correspondence, accounts, and financial accounting data

## Source anchors

- `ACT` s.151 — the Key Terms above, verbatim from the Interpretation section.
- `ACT` s.43 — customs house agent licensing; s.44 — agent to produce authority.
- `ACT` s.139 — bribes; s.133 — impersonation of an officer: cited as the conduct
  boundaries; penalty amounts banned (C4.4).
- `CPC-LAW` — the GRA deck's training-and-certification account of agency duties, as
  the practice-side frame.
- The legal-anchors fact sheet (planned): when written, it grounds the conduct
  instruments and the CHA licensing route's administrative detail.

**Fact-check caution.** A named code of conduct, professional-body rules or a licensing
regime's administrative steps must wait for the legal-anchors fact sheet — until then,
the Act's provisions and the deck's description are the anchors. Do not state a
professional body's name or a code's contents from memory.
