# LESSON BRIEF 7.1 — Structure of the Harmonised System and the Ghana tariff

Course 3: Classification, Valuation, Origin and Duty · difficulty `intermediate`
Module 7: Harmonised System Classification · lesson 1 of 4 · module 7 of 15
Proposed slug: `structure-of-the-harmonised-system-and-the-ghana-tariff`

## Summary

Every duty number a learner will ever quote lives in a hierarchy: sections, chapters,
headings, sub-headings — and beneath them, Ghana's Tariff Subheadings with their rate
columns. Act 891 imposes duty at the rate the Harmonised System specifies and defines
the HS as the convention nomenclature the whole structure comes from. This lesson
teaches the structure itself, using the supplied tariff workbook as the worked text.

## Learning Objectives

State the HS hierarchy — sections, chapters, headings, sub-headings — and how Ghana's TSN layer sits beneath it.
Identify the tariff workbook's structure: 377 tables of Heading, TSN, Commodity Description, SU, CET ID, VAT and IE columns.
Explain how Act 891 s.1(1) ties the duty rate to the Harmonised System, and what the Act's HS definition incorporates.
Navigate a tariff table row and name each column's role.

## Key Terms

Harmonised Commodity Description and Coding System :: the nomenclature comprising the headings, sub-headings and their related numerical codes, the section, chapter and sub-heading notes and the general rules for the interpretation of the Harmonised System contained in the text of the International Convention on the Harmonised Commodity Description and Coding System approved by the Customs Co-operation Council on 14th June, 1983 and the relevant Schedules attached to it
tariff :: the Tariff Schedule of Ghana
duty :: customs duty, excise duty, tax or other charges collectible on imported or exported goods

## Source anchors

- `ACT` s.151 — the Key Terms above, verbatim from the Interpretation section.
- `ACT` s.1(1) — duties and taxes imposed at the rate specified in the Harmonised
  System; s.1(2)(a) — any other tax, duty or levy prescribed under another enactment.
- `TARIFF` — `output/docx/HSCODE/HS FINAL 2017-15-506.xlsx`: 377 sheets; each data
  table headed Heading / TSN / Commodity Description / SU / CET ID / VAT / IE (verified
  against the workbook, 2026-11-16). Cite sheets and rows by table number.

**Fact-check caution.** The tariff edition rule (plan decision 4, spec C4.2) applies to
every rate: the supplied schedule is the 2024 refresh of a 2017 base and the two differ
on excise lines. Teach the structure and the columns; mark every rate "illustrative of
the structure — verify the rate in force". Never carry a rate from memory.
