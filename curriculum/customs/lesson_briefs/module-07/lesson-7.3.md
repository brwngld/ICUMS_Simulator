# LESSON BRIEF 7.3 — Classification workflow, product descriptions and advance rulings

Course 3: Classification, Valuation, Origin and Duty · difficulty `intermediate`
Module 7: Harmonised System Classification · lesson 3 of 4 · module 7 of 15
Proposed slug: `classification-workflow-product-descriptions-and-advance-rulings`

## Summary

Good classification starts before the tariff book opens: in what the goods actually
are, as a precise product description. The workflow runs from description to candidate
headings to notes and rules to a decided TSN — and where the answer is uncertain, the
Act lets a trader ask customs in advance and hold the written ruling. This lesson
teaches the working method and the advance ruling's legal force.

## Learning Objectives

Describe the classification workflow: precise product description, candidate headings, notes check, GRI application, TSN decision.
State what makes a product description classification-grade — composition, function, form, packaging — and what makes it useless.
Explain the customs advance ruling under section 12: who applies, what it binds, and what it covers.
Connect the ruling to real practice: when a trader should seek one and what it costs them to ignore it.

## Key Terms

customs advance ruling :: a binding written statement issued by customs that interprets and applies customs laws and regulations to a specific set of facts upon application by a trade about a prospective transaction
tariff :: the Tariff Schedule of Ghana
Harmonised Commodity Description and Coding System :: the nomenclature comprising the headings, sub-headings and their related numerical codes, the section, chapter and sub-heading notes and the general rules for the interpretation of the Harmonised System contained in the text of the International Convention on the Harmonised Commodity Description and Coding System approved by the Customs Co-operation Council on 14th June, 1983 and the relevant Schedules attached to it

## Source anchors

- `ACT` s.151 — the Key Terms above, verbatim from the Interpretation section.
- `ACT` s.12 — customs advance ruling: quote the provision's own framing of the
  ruling's effect (the application process detail that lives in Regulations is not
  stated here unless the Act states it).
- `TARIFF` — the workbook, as the classification destination the workflow lands in.
- The instruments fact sheet: when written, it grounds the GRI references the
  workflow invokes.
