# LESSON BRIEF 9.3 — Certificates of origin and documentary evidence

Course 3: Classification, Valuation, Origin and Duty · difficulty `intermediate`
Module 9: Rules of Origin and Trade Preferences · lesson 3 of 4 · module 9 of 15
Proposed slug: `certificates-of-origin-and-documentary-evidence`

## Summary

A preference claim is only as strong as its paper: the certificate of origin, issued
or attested by the authorised body, ties the goods to the rule that grants the
preference. This lesson teaches what the certificate must show, who issues it, how it
travels with the consignment, and what the declaration does with it.

## Learning Objectives

State what a certificate of origin evidences and who may issue or attest it, from the sources' own account.
Identify the certificate's data points — exporter, consignee, goods description, origin criterion, reference to the invoice — and why each matters.
Explain how the certificate attaches to the declaration and what the officer checks.
Describe the evidence chain for a claim: invoice, transport document, certificate, declaration.

## Key Terms

export :: the act of taking out or causing to be taken out any goods from the country
import :: to bring or cause goods to be brought into the country
declaration :: a statutory form filed or to be filed with respect to imported or exported goods at the time of entry or export
record :: any information kept in the ordinary course of business that pertains to any customs activity, in the nature of a statement, declaration, document, data, book, paper, correspondence, accounts, and financial accounting data

## Source anchors

- `ACT` s.151 — the Key Terms above, verbatim from the Interpretation section.
- `AFU` — the annexes' documentary-evidence provisions, as the preference-side
  certificate requirements; quote with annex references.
- `CPC-TT` — the shipping deck's document slides, as the transport-document context.
- `ACT` s.14 — documents to be submitted, as the declaration-side anchor.

**Fact-check caution.** Do not name Ghana's issuing chambers or authorities for
certificates of origin — the institutions fact sheet is not yet written. Describe the
issuer as "the authorised issuing body" unless the supplied sources name it. Certificate
formats vary by agreement: state only what the cited annex or source shows.
