# LESSON BRIEF 5.2 — Unique Consignment References and declaration preparation

Course 2: Customs Procedures, Electronic Declarations and Import Controls · difficulty `beginner-intermediate`
Module 5: Electronic Customs Systems and Declarations · lesson 2 of 4 · module 5 of 15
Proposed slug: `unique-consignment-references-and-declaration-preparation`

## Summary

Before a consignment can be declared, the system needs to know which arrival it
belongs to: the unique consignment reference ties a declaration to the goods that
actually landed. Act 891 defines the declaration's statutory nature, the declarant who
makes it, and the pre-entry that may be lodged before the goods arrive. This lesson
teaches what a declaration must carry, how preparation differs from lodgement, and
where the UCR fits in that chain.

## Learning Objectives

State what the declaration is as a statutory form and what its particulars exist to prove.
Explain the declarant's identity options — owner, importer, exporter, or agent — and what each implies.
Describe the pre-entry (s.50): lodging before importation, and the benefit it buys at the border.
Explain the unique consignment reference's role as the link between a declaration and the reported goods, as a rule of the electronic environment rather than a screen.

## Key Terms

declaration :: a statutory form filed or to be filed with respect to imported or exported goods at the time of entry or export
declarant :: a person who makes a declaration for customs purposes, and who is the owner of the goods, the importer, exporter, or the agent
pre-entry :: the entry of goods prior to their importation into Ghana
electronic declaration :: a declaration made electronically to the Commissioner-General, whether personally or on behalf of any person as agent of the particulars of a consignment of goods imported or exported or any other declaration authorised by the Commissioner-General in connection with the import or export of goods under this Act

**Fact-check caution.** C7 applies in full: teach the rule — the declaration must be
tied to a consignment, the particulars must match the reported goods, missing
documents have consequences under s.51 — without describing any screen. Do not state
UCR format rules, number structures or system behaviours; the systems fact sheet will
carry those when written.
