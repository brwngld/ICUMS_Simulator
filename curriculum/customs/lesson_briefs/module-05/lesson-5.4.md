# LESSON BRIEF 5.4 — Workflow statuses, validation, payment, release and record keeping

Course 2: Customs Procedures, Electronic Declarations and Import Controls · difficulty `beginner-intermediate`

Proposed slug: `workflow-statuses-validation-payment-release-and-record-keeping`

## Summary

An electronic declaration moves through states: lodged, validated, assessed, paid,
released. Each state is a legal condition, not a screen label — validation checks the
particulars, payment settles the duty, release applies section 6's conditions, and
record keeping keeps the whole trail afterwards. This lesson closes Module 5 by
teaching the declaration's legal lifecycle and the records it leaves behind.

## Learning Objectives

Explain validation as a legal check: the declaration's particulars must match the documents and the goods.
State the liability and calculation provisions (ss.71–73) that the payment step settles — naming the hook, not the arithmetic.
Apply the release conditions of section 6(1) to an electronically lodged declaration.
Describe the record-keeping duty (s.9) and the post-clearance audit window (s.7) that the electronic trail serves.

## Key Terms

electronic declaration :: a declaration made electronically to the Commissioner-General, whether personally or on behalf of any person as agent of the particulars of a consignment of goods imported or exported or any other declaration authorised by the Commissioner-General in connection with the import or export of goods under this Act
electronic record :: a record created, generated, sent, communicated, received, or stored by electronic means
duty :: customs duty, excise duty, tax or other charges collectible on imported or exported goods
record :: any information kept in the ordinary course of business that pertains to any customs activity, in the nature of a statement, declaration, document, data, book, paper, correspondence, accounts, and financial accounting data

## Source anchors

- `ACT` s.151 — the Key Terms above, verbatim from the Interpretation section.
- `ACT` s.6 — release of goods (the conditions, verbatim).
- `ACT` ss.71–73 — liability for duty, calculation of duty, payment of duty: cited as
  the payment step's legal basis; the arithmetic is Module 8's.
- `ACT` ss.7 and 9 — post-clearance audit and record keeping, as the after-life of the
  declaration.
- TIMETABLE — the programme's workflow teaching topics.

**Fact-check caution.** C7 governs: statuses are taught as legal conditions (what must
be true before goods release), never as named screens, colours or buttons. Do not
state ICUMS status names, payment channels or message formats; the named-systems fact
sheet will carry those when written.
