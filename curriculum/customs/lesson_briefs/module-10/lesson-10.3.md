# LESSON BRIEF 10.3 — Exemptions, abatements, refunds and drawback

Course 4: Warehousing, Special Movements, Risk and Examination · difficulty `intermediate-advanced`
Module 10: Warehousing, Reliefs and Revenue Adjustments · lesson 3 of 4 · module 10 of 15
Proposed slug: `exemptions-abatements-refunds-and-drawback`

## Summary

Not every import pays full duty: the law exempts some goods outright, abates some
duty, refunds what was overpaid, and returns duty on goods imported to be processed
for export. Act 891 provides each relief — exemptions (s.2), refund and remission
(s.104), abatement (s.105), drawback (s.106) — and gives removal articles their own
exemption. This lesson teaches the relief family and the discipline each claim needs.

## Learning Objectives

State the exemption provisions: exempt goods in the Harmonised System, scheduled persons and organisations, and the removal article (s.2(1)–(3), using the Act's removal-article definition).
Distinguish remission, refund and abatement as s.104 and s.105 provide them.
Explain drawback (s.106): duty returned on goods imported for processing and re-export.
Identify what a claimant must evidence for each relief, from the provisions' own terms.

## Key Terms

removal article :: movable personal belongings or effects, domestic appliances, household provisions normally kept in stock, collectors pieces, pet animals and any tools of trade other than agricultural, commercial or industrial plant necessary for the exercise of the calling, trade or profession of any person referred to in this Act, but does not include arms, ammunition, motor vehicles, spare parts and building materials
duty :: customs duty, excise duty, tax or other charges collectible on imported or exported goods
tariff :: the Tariff Schedule of Ghana
raw material :: goods from which other goods are capable of being manufactured and any residue from any process of manufacture

## Source anchors

- `ACT` s.151 — the Key Terms above, verbatim from the Interpretation section; note
  the removal-article definition's own exclusions (arms, ammunition, motor vehicles,
  spare parts, building materials) — quote them, they are the definition's teeth.
- `ACT` s.2 — exempt duties and taxes: s.2(1) exempt goods specified in the HS,
  s.2(2) persons/organisations/institutions in the Schedules, s.2(3) removal article.
- `ACT` s.104 — refund and remission; s.105 — abatement of duty; s.106 — drawback.
- `TARIFF` — the workbook's exemption-relevant notes, where used, cited by table.

**Fact-check caution.** Never list an exempted good, person or institution from
memory: the exemption lives in the HS specification and the Schedules the Act points
to, and the learner must be taught to look it up, not to recite it. Any relief rate
carries the spec C4.2 label.
