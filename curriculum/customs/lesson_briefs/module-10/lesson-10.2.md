# LESSON BRIEF 10.2 — State warehouse, forfeiture, auction and allocation procedures

Proposed slug: `state-warehouse-forfeiture-auction-and-allocation-procedures`

## Summary

When goods are never cleared, the law does not wait forever: they go to the State
warehouse, and in due course they are auctioned and the proceeds applied. Act 891
provides the deposit (s.53), the auctioned-goods procedure (s.93) and the
seizure-and-forfeiture machinery (ss.124–126). This lesson walks the process — and
uses the anonymised Form 80 allocation specimen as the document the learner will
actually meet.

## Learning Objectives

State the State warehouse's purpose (the s.151 definition) and the deposit trigger (s.53).
Describe the auctioned-goods procedure (s.93) and what the auction is for.
Walk the seizure-and-forfeiture machinery: seizure (s.124), claims for seized goods (s.125), disposal of goods (s.126).
Read the auction-allocation letter specimen: who allocates what, and to whom — as a training document, not a template to copy.

## Key Terms

State warehouse :: a place approved by the Commissioner-General for the deposit of uncustomed, un-entered, un-examined, abandoned, detained or seized goods for the security of the goods or the payment of duties that may be due
uncustomed goods :: goods liable to duty on which the full duties due have not been paid, and goods, whether liable to duty or not, that are imported or exported or in any way dealt with contrary to the provisions of this Act in relation to customs
prohibited goods :: goods whose importation or exportation is prohibited by law
cargo :: goods other than stores or passenger or crew baggage

## Source anchors

- `ACT` s.151 — the Key Terms above, verbatim from the Interpretation section.
- `ACT` s.52 — uncleared goods; s.53 — deposit of goods in the State warehouse.
- `ACT` s.93 — auctioned goods.
- `ACT` ss.124–126 — seizure and forfeiture, claims for seized goods, disposal of
  goods.
- `SPECIMEN` — `output/docx/Auction-Allocation-Letter-SPECIMEN.pdf`: the anonymised
  Form 80 allocation letter, produced as a training specimen; teach its structure,
  not its parties.

**Fact-check caution.** Auction fees, storage rent and time limits are figures and
deadlines: state them only where the cited sections or the specimen state them, and
label each per spec C4.1. The specimen is anonymised — never present its parties as
real. Penalty amounts stay banned (spec C4.4).
