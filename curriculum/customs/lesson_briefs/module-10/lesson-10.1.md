# LESSON BRIEF 10.1 — Customs warehousing, bonds, entries and removals

Course 4: Warehousing, Special Movements, Risk and Examination · difficulty `intermediate-advanced`
Module 10: Warehousing, Reliefs and Revenue Adjustments · lesson 1 of 4 · module 10 of 15
Proposed slug: `customs-warehousing-bonds-entries-and-removals`

## Summary

A bonded warehouse is duty suspended in a building: goods sit secured under the
warehouse keeper's licence and bond, entered and removed only as the Act allows. Act
891's warehousing machinery — ss.79–88 — was introduced in Module 4; this lesson works
it as a regime: the licence, the security, the entry, the removal and the keeper's
responsibilities throughout.

## Learning Objectives

State the licensing conditions (s.47(1)) and what s.79 adds for a private bonded warehouse, including the keeper's bond.
Describe the records the warehouse keeper must keep (s.80) and why the record is the regime's spine.
Walk a removal: from transit shed to warehouse or between warehouses (s.85), and the entry of warehoused goods (s.88).
Identify the regime's failure modes and the Act's response: goods not duly removed (s.81), unlawful access (s.82), unauthorised removal (s.83), destruction and stealing (s.84).

## Key Terms

private bonded warehouse :: a building or place appointed by the Commissioner-General and licensed to be a place where goods to be warehoused are secured
warehouse keeper :: the person licensed to operate a private bonded warehouse
bonded carrier :: a carrier licensed in accordance with this Act to transport bonded goods within the country
record :: any information kept in the ordinary course of business that pertains to any customs activity, in the nature of a statement, declaration, document, data, book, paper, correspondence, accounts, and financial accounting data

## Source anchors

- `ACT` s.151 — the Key Terms above, verbatim from the Interpretation section.
- `ACT` s.47 — warehouse: licensing, bond, fees, licence form and revocation.
- `ACT` ss.79–88 — the regime: s.79 private bonded warehouse; s.80 records; s.81 goods
  not duly removed; s.82 unlawful access; s.83 unauthorised removal; s.84 destruction
  and stealing; s.85 removal from transit shed or between warehouses; s.86 delivery in
  special circumstances; s.87 stowing; s.88 entry of warehoused goods. Cite each claim
  to its section.
- `ACT` ss.108–111 — the security machinery (forms of security, surety, release and
  cancellation of guarantee, validity of bond), which s.47(1)(b)'s bond requirement
  plugs into.
