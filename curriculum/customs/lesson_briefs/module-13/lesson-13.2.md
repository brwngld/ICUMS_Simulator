# LESSON BRIEF 13.2 — Public-health, standards and high-risk product controls

Course 5: Advanced Border Controls, Compliance and Professional Practice · difficulty `advanced`
Module 13: Special Goods and Advanced Border Controls · lesson 2 of 4 · module 13 of 15
Proposed slug: `public-health-standards-and-high-risk-product-controls`

## Summary

The border is a public-health checkpoint: foods, drugs, cosmetics, medical devices and
standards-regulated products move only through the agencies' controls, and high-risk
categories attract inspection before release. Act 851 gives FDA its statute; the GSA
decks give standards their practice. This lesson teaches the high-risk product map and
the controls a learner must expect — pulling Module 6's threads into one picture.

## Learning Objectives

Identify the high-risk product categories the agencies control — food, drugs, cosmetics, medical devices, regulated standards goods — from the decks' own lists.
State the control each category faces at the border: permit, inspection, certification or testing.
Explain how agency controls change the clearance sequence for high-risk goods: release waits on the agency's satisfaction.
Connect Act 851's public-health purpose to the border controls the learner sees.

## Key Terms

prohibited goods :: goods whose importation or exportation is prohibited by law
restricted goods :: goods whose importation or exportation is restricted by law
electronic record :: a record created, generated, sent, communicated, received, or stored by electronic means
