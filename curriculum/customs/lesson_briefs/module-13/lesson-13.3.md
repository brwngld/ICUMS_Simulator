# LESSON BRIEF 13.3 — Motor vehicles, used goods and specialised valuation issues

Course 5: Advanced Border Controls, Compliance and Professional Practice · difficulty `advanced`
Module 13: Special Goods and Advanced Border Controls · lesson 3 of 4 · module 13 of 15
Proposed slug: `motor-vehicles-used-goods-and-specialised-valuation-issues`

## Summary

Used vehicles are the border's hardest ordinary case: age and value rules, prohibited
vehicles, forfeiture for overstaying, and a valuation method of their own. Act 891
devotes ss.55–61 to motor vehicles — tax, licensing duties, importation, prohibition,
forfeiture, valuation of used vehicles and dealer details. This lesson teaches the
vehicle-specific machinery and the valuation issues that make used vehicles special.

## Learning Objectives

State the vehicle machinery's parts: motor vehicle tax (s.55), the licensing authority's duties (s.56), and importation rules (s.57).
Explain prohibited vehicles (s.58) and forfeiture of overstayed vehicles (s.59).
Describe the used-vehicle valuation approach (s.60) and how it differs from standard transaction-value work.
Identify the dealer-information duty (s.61) and what it gives customs.

## Key Terms

motor vehicle :: a mechanically propelled vehicle for use on a road other than a cycle, motor cycle, side-car or other similar vehicle
vehicle :: a conveyance for the transport by land of goods or persons
duty :: customs duty, excise duty, tax or other charges collectible on imported or exported goods
customs value of imported goods :: the value of goods, determined in accordance with this Act
