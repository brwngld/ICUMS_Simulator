# LESSON BRIEF 2.4 — Incoterms and the allocation of cost, risk and responsibility

Course 1: Customs Foundations, Documents and the Clearance Ecosystem · difficulty `beginner`
Module 2: Trade, Shipping and Clearance Documents · lesson 4 of 4 · module 2 of 15
Proposed slug: `incoterms-and-the-allocation-of-cost-risk-and-responsibility`

## Summary

Incoterms decide where the seller's job ends and the buyer's begins: who pays the
freight, who carries the risk in transit, and whose declaration the goods arrive
under. Customs reads the delivery term because it tells the officer which cost lines
belong in the customs value and which do not. This lesson teaches the eleven
Incoterms 2020 rules, the three the Ghana trade lane uses most — FOB, CFR and CIF —
and how the delivery term on the invoice maps to the customs entry.

## Learning Objectives

State what an Incoterm allocates: delivery place, cost split, and risk transfer point.
Distinguish the two families — any-mode rules (EXW, FCA, CPT, CIP, DAP, DPU, DDP) and sea/inland-waterway rules (FAS, FOB, CFR, CIF).
Quote the delivery and risk-transfer points of FOB, CFR and CIF from the deck's definitions.
Explain why the delivery term on the commercial invoice matters to the customs entry.

## Key Terms

FOB :: Free On Board — the seller delivers the goods to the buyer on board the vessel nominated by the buyer at the named port of shipment, or procures goods already so delivered; risk of loss or damage transfers when the goods are on board the vessel, and the buyer bears all costs from that moment onwards (Incoterms 2020, quoted from the CPC-TT deck)
CFR :: Cost and Freight — the seller delivers the goods on board the vessel nominated by the buyer at the named port of shipment; risk transfers when the goods are on board the vessel even though the seller pays freight to the destination port (Incoterms 2020, quoted from the CPC-TT deck)
CIF :: Cost, Insurance and Freight — as CFR with the seller additionally contracting for insurance against the buyer's risk of loss or damage in transit (Incoterms 2020, quoted from the CPC-TT deck)

## Source anchors

- `CPC-TT` — the Incoterms section: the deck lists all eleven rules and splits them
  into "Any mode of transport" (EXW, FCA, CPT, CIP, DAP, DPU, DDP) and "Incoterms for
  sea and inland waterway transport" (FAS, FOB, CFR, CIF), then defines each rule on
  its own slide. The Key Terms quote the deck's own wording for FOB and CFR; CIF's
  rendering above compresses the deck's slide — verify the CIF slide's exact wording
  before quoting it in the draft.
- `ACT` s.151 — buying commission and selling commission, as the two commission
  definitions that reappear in valuation (Module 8); mention only if the lesson needs
  them for the cost-allocation bridge.
- WEB-verified (2026-11-16): the current set is Incoterms 2020, published by the
  International Chamber of Commerce; "Incoterms 2020" is the correct label to use.
