# LESSON BRIEF 4.4 — Suspense procedures and movement under customs control

Course 2: Customs Procedures, Electronic Declarations and Import Controls · difficulty `beginner-intermediate`
Module 4: Customs Regimes and Core Procedures · lesson 4 of 4 · module 4 of 15
Proposed slug: `suspense-procedures-and-movement-under-customs-control`

## Summary

Suspense procedures hold goods in a legal state where duty is not yet due: bonded
warehousing stores them under the Commissioner-General's licence, and goods move
between sheds and warehouses under customs control without leaving it. Act 891
devotes sections 79 to 88 to this machinery — licensing, record keeping, removal,
delivery in special circumstances — and defines the places it happens in. This lesson
teaches the suspense idea and the movement rules that keep goods inside customs
control until duty or export settles their position.

## Learning Objectives

Explain what a suspense procedure is: duty deferred, goods still under customs control.
State the three licensing conditions for a private bonded warehouse under section 47(1) and what section 79 adds.
Identify the movement rules — removal from a transit shed or between warehouses (s.85) and entry of warehoused goods (s.88) — and the control that accompanies each.
Describe what happens when the rules break: goods not duly removed (s.81), unlawful access (s.82), unauthorised removal (s.83).

## Key Terms

private bonded warehouse :: a building or place appointed by the Commissioner-General and licensed to be a place where goods to be warehoused are secured
warehouse keeper :: the person licensed to operate a private bonded warehouse
transit-shed :: a building in a customs-controlled area, designated by the Commissioner-General by notice in writing, for the deposit of un-entered, un-declared goods
customs-controlled area :: a place in the country, designated by the Commissioner-General, where the Authority has control over goods, persons and conveyances
bonded carrier :: a carrier licensed in accordance with this Act to transport bonded goods within the country

## Source anchors

- `ACT` s.151 — the Key Terms above, verbatim from the Interpretation section.
- `ACT` s.5(1)(d) — storage and processing of imported goods under bond, as the
  customs-controlled-area activity the suspense regime lives inside.
- `ACT` s.47 — warehouse (licensing, bond, fees, revocation) as taught in lesson 1.3;
  cited here as the entry gate to ss.79–88.
- `ACT` ss.79–88 — the warehousing machinery: s.79 private bonded warehouse; s.80
  records to be kept in bonded warehouse; s.81 goods not duly removed; s.82 unlawful
  access; s.83 unauthorised removal; s.84 destruction and stealing; s.85 removal from
  transit shed or between warehouses; s.86 delivery in special circumstances; s.87
  stowing of goods; s.88 entry of warehoused goods. The draft names the sections it
  uses and cites each claim to its section.
