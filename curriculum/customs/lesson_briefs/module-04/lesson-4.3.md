# LESSON BRIEF 4.3 — Temporary importation, re-export and re-importation

Course 2: Customs Procedures, Electronic Declarations and Import Controls · difficulty `beginner-intermediate`
Module 4: Customs Regimes and Core Procedures · lesson 3 of 4 · module 4 of 15
Proposed slug: `temporary-importation-re-export-and-re-importation`

## Summary

Not every import stays. Equipment comes for a job and leaves; Ghanaian goods go out
for processing and come back; exported goods return unchanged. Act 891 provides the
procedures for each case — temporary admission, inward processing, re-importation in
the same state and after outward processing — and the duty treatment turns on which
one the goods actually follow. This lesson teaches the family and the duty logic that
separates its members.

## Learning Objectives

State what temporary admission (s.75) allows and what duty consequence it defers.
Explain inward processing (s.76): goods imported to be processed and re-exported.
Distinguish re-importation in the same state (s.77) from re-importation after outward processing (s.78).
Identify the re-exportation of commercial goods (s.89) as the exit route when goods under a deferral regime leave.

## Key Terms

import :: to bring or cause goods to be brought into the country
export :: the act of taking out or causing to be taken out any goods from the country
duty :: customs duty, excise duty, tax or other charges collectible on imported or exported goods
manufacture :: to make, produce, or cause to be made or produced, goods
raw material :: goods from which other goods are capable of being manufactured and any residue from any process of manufacture

## Source anchors

- `ACT` s.151 — the Key Terms above, verbatim from the Interpretation section

- `ACT` s.75 — temporary admission: quote the provision's own framing.
- `ACT` s.76 — inward processing; s.77 — re-importation in same state; s.78 —
  re-importation after outward processing.
- `ACT` s.89 — re-exportation of commercial goods.
- `TIMETABLE` — the programme's treatment of the temporary-importation topic.

**Fact-check caution.** The Act sections above are the anchor. Security conditions,
time limits and application forms for these regimes may exist in the Regulations
(L.I. 2248) — do not state a specific period or condition unless the supplied sources
state it; write "as prescribed by Regulations" where the Act says so.
