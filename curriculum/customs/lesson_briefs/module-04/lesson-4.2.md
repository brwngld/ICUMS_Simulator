# LESSON BRIEF 4.2 — Customs Procedure Codes and declaration types

Course 2: Customs Procedures, Electronic Declarations and Import Controls · difficulty `beginner-intermediate`
Module 4: Customs Regimes and Core Procedures · lesson 2 of 4 · module 4 of 15
Proposed slug: `customs-procedure-codes-and-declaration-types`

## Summary

The declaration carries a code that tells customs which legal procedure the goods are
entering — home use, transit, warehousing, temporary admission — and each code carries
its own conditions, documents and duty treatment. Act 891 defines the declaration and
the pre-entry that may precede it. This lesson teaches the learner to read a procedure
code as the label of a legal regime, not a formality.

## Learning Objectives

Explain what the procedure code on a declaration selects: the regime, its conditions and its duty treatment.
State what a declaration is under the Act, who may make it (the declarant), and when it is filed.
Describe the pre-entry under section 50: goods entered before importation, and what that achieves.
Identify the declaration types a learner will meet in practice — import, export, transit and warehousing entries — from the procedure each performs.

## Key Terms

declaration :: a statutory form filed or to be filed with respect to imported or exported goods at the time of entry or export
declarant :: a person who makes a declaration for customs purposes, and who is the owner of the goods, the importer, exporter, or the agent
pre-entry :: the entry of goods prior to their importation into Ghana
electronic declaration :: a declaration made electronically to the Commissioner-General, whether personally or on behalf of any person as agent of the particulars of a consignment of goods imported or exported or any other declaration authorised by the Commissioner-General in connection with the import or export of goods under this Act
time of entry :: the Act's entry-timing provision (s.48) — see the anchor note; "time of entry" is fixed by section 48's own terms rather than the Interpretation section

## Source anchors

- `ACT` s.151 — declaration, declarant, pre-entry and electronic declaration, verbatim
  from the Interpretation section.
- `ACT` s.48 — time of entry: cite the section for the entry moment (s.151 does not
  define "time of entry"; the draft states the entry timing from s.48 itself and labels
  the source accordingly).
- `ACT` s.49 — declaration; s.50 — pre-entry; s.51 — missing or incomplete documents.
- TIMETABLE — the proficiency programme's CPC teaching topics, as the emphasis anchor.
- The procedures named in the Act's own sections give the code families their meaning:
  home use (s.74), temporary admission (s.75), warehousing (ss.79–88), transit (s.95),
  transhipment (s.96) — cite these as the regimes the codes select.
