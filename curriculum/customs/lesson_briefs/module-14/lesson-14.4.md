# LESSON BRIEF 14.4 — Complex multi-agency clearance and enforcement cases

Course 5: Advanced Border Controls, Compliance and Professional Practice · difficulty `advanced`
Module 14: Advanced Compliance and Case Management · lesson 4 of 4 · module 14 of 15
Proposed slug: `complex-multi-agency-clearance-and-enforcement-cases`

## Summary

The hardest cases combine everything at once: an agency hold, a valuation query, a
seizure and a dispute running in parallel. Act 891's seizure-and-disposal machinery
(ss.124–127) gives enforcement its spine, and the case-management skill is holding
each legal thread to its own provision. This lesson works complex case patterns —
pulling the programme's threads into disciplined case handling.

## Learning Objectives

Assemble a complex case map: the agency thread, the valuation thread, the enforcement thread — each with its provision and its clock.
State the seizure-and-disposal machinery as the enforcement spine: seizure (s.124), claims (s.125), disposal (s.126), prohibitions (s.127).
Apply the coordination lesson of Module 6 to a case where an agency hold and a customs query run together.
Manage the case's paper: records (s.9), notices, and the dispute rungs (ss.12–13) used deliberately.

## Key Terms

prohibited goods :: goods whose importation or exportation is prohibited by law
uncustomed goods :: goods liable to duty on which the full duties due have not been paid, and goods, whether liable to duty or not, that are imported or exported or in any way dealt with contrary to the provisions of this Act in relation to customs
record :: any information kept in the ordinary course of business that pertains to any customs activity, in the nature of a statement, declaration, document, data, book, paper, correspondence, accounts, and financial accounting data
duty :: customs duty, excise duty, tax or other charges collectible on imported or exported goods

## Source anchors

- `ACT` s.151 — the Key Terms above, verbatim from the Interpretation section.
- `ACT` ss.124–127 — seizure and forfeiture, claims for seized goods, disposal of
  goods, prohibitions.
- `ACT` ss.12–13 — advance ruling and objection, as the dispute rungs.
- `CPC-RISK` and `CPC-FDA` — the decks' case-relevant sections, cited by slide.
