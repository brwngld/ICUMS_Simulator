# LESSON BRIEF 14.3 — Advance rulings, objections, appeals and dispute management

Course 5: Advanced Border Controls, Compliance and Professional Practice · difficulty `advanced`
Module 14: Advanced Compliance and Case Management · lesson 3 of 4 · module 14 of 15
Proposed slug: `advance-rulings-objections-appeals-and-dispute-management`

## Summary

Disagreement with customs has a lawful ladder: the advance ruling that settles a
question before it arises, the objection against a decision already made, and the
appeal beyond it. Act 891 provides the ruling (s.12) and the objection (s.13). This
lesson teaches the ladder, the timing each rung carries, and how a professional
manages a dispute rather than merely enduring one.

## Learning Objectives

State what the customs advance ruling (s.12) is and what it binds, and when a trader should seek one.
Explain the objection (s.13): what decisions it addresses and how it is made.
Sequence the dispute ladder: ruling, objection, and the appeal routes the Act and other laws provide.
Manage a dispute as a professional: the records that support the position (s.9), and the posture that avoids the dispute entirely.

## Key Terms

customs advance ruling :: a binding written statement issued by customs that interprets and applies customs laws and regulations to a specific set of facts upon application by a trade about a prospective transaction
record :: any information kept in the ordinary course of business that pertains to any customs activity, in the nature of a statement, declaration, document, data, book, paper, correspondence, accounts, and financial accounting data
proper officer :: an officer whose right or duty it is to exact the performance of or to perform the act referred to
duty :: customs duty, excise duty, tax or other charges collectible on imported or exported goods
