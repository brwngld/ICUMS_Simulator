# LESSON BRIEF 6.4 — Managing multi-agency approvals, holds and discrepancies

Course 2: Customs Procedures, Electronic Declarations and Import Controls · difficulty `beginner-intermediate`
Module 6: MDA Requirements and Import Controls · lesson 4 of 4 · module 6 of 15
Proposed slug: `managing-multi-agency-approvals-holds-and-discrepancies`

## Summary

When an agency hold meets a customs query, the clearance stalls in two systems at
once — and the professional's job is to work both threads without confusing either.
Act 891 supplies the customs-side machinery (prohibited and restricted goods, the
uncustomed-goods state, release conditions), the decks supply the agency side. This
lesson closes Module 6 by teaching the holds-and-discrepancies playbook: what each
hold means, who clears it, and how the clearance resumes.

## Learning Objectives

Map the hold types a multi-agency clearance meets — permit outstanding, inspection pending, documents discrepant — and the provision behind each.
State the customs-side rules that govern a stuck clearance: release conditions (s.6), missing or incomplete documents (s.51), and the uncleared-goods consequence (s.52).
Explain a discrepancy's lifecycle: found, evidenced, corrected, and re-submitted — each step traced to its provision.
Describe the escalation discipline: which thread belongs to which agency, and what the agent does on each.

## Key Terms

prohibited goods :: goods whose importation or exportation is prohibited by law
restricted goods :: goods whose importation or exportation is restricted by law
uncustomed goods :: goods liable to duty on which the full duties due have not been paid, and goods, whether liable to duty or not, that are imported or exported or in any way dealt with contrary to the provisions of this Act in relation to customs
declaration :: a statutory form filed or to be filed with respect to imported or exported goods at the time of entry or export

## Source anchors

- `ACT` s.151 — the Key Terms above, verbatim from the Interpretation section.
- `ACT` s.6 — release conditions; s.51 — missing or incomplete documents; s.52 —
  uncleared goods.
- `ACT` s.37 — prohibited and restricted goods, as the frame an agency hold sits in.
- `CPC-GSA` and `CPC-FDA` — the decks' hold/inspection content, cited by slide.

**Fact-check caution.** Hold types are taught as legal states, not screen labels —
C7 applies. Do not state agency turnaround times, portal behaviours or named hold
codes; the decks and the named-systems fact sheet (when written) carry those. Every
customs-side claim cites its section.
