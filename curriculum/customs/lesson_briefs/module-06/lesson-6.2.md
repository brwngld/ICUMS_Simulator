# LESSON BRIEF 6.2 — Ghana Standards Authority import requirements and inspection

Proposed slug: `ghana-standards-authority-import-requirements-and-inspection`

## Summary

The Ghana Standards Authority guards what enters the country's markets: certain
imports need GSA approval, inspection or certification before customs releases them.
The GSA decks set out the authority's mandate at the ports and the import requirements
it enforces. This lesson teaches what GSA requires, how its inspection meets the
clearance, and which subsidiary instruments the decks cite for its powers.

## Learning Objectives

State GSA's mandate at the ports and borders as the decks present it.
Identify the import requirements GSA applies — product standards, labelling, certification — from the deck's own list.
Describe how GSA inspection connects to a customs clearance and what evidence the clearance sees.
Name the subsidiary instruments the decks cite (LI 1541, LI 1586, LI 1932, NRCD 173) as the legal anchors for standards controls.

## Key Terms

prohibited goods :: goods whose importation or exportation is prohibited by law
restricted goods :: goods whose importation or exportation is restricted by law
raw material :: goods from which other goods are capable of being manufactured and any residue from any process of manufacture

## Source anchors

- `ACT` s.151 — the Key Terms above, verbatim from the Interpretation section.
- `CPC-GSA` — the GRA-course GSA deck: mandate at the ports and borders, import
  requirements, and the inspection flow. Quote its lists with slide numbers.
- `GSA-NOTES` — the GSA inspectorate deck (March 2022): requirements for customs
  business, as the second anchor for the same content.
- The legal-anchors fact sheet (`ghana-legal-anchors.md`, planned): when written, it
  will carry what LI 1541, LI 1586, LI 1932 and NRCD 173 actually say.

**Fact-check caution.** The legal-anchors fact sheet is not yet written: name the four
subsidiary instruments as instruments the decks cite, and do not state what any of
them provides. Everything else in this lesson quotes the two GSA decks; where they
disagree, say so in `FACTS & SOURCES` rather than picking one silently.
