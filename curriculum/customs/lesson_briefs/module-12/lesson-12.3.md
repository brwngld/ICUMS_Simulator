# LESSON BRIEF 12.3 — Risk profiles, indicators, intelligence and targeting

Course 4: Warehousing, Special Movements, Risk and Examination · difficulty `intermediate-advanced`
Module 12: Customs Risk Management and Examination · lesson 3 of 4 · module 12 of 15
Proposed slug: `risk-profiles-indicators-intelligence-and-targeting`

## Summary

A risk profile is the register's operational face: criteria that decide, before a
consignment arrives, which ones deserve a look. Indicators are the tell-tales;
intelligence is where they come from; targeting is applying the profile to real
declarations. Act 891's confidentiality rule governs all of it. This lesson teaches
how profiles are built and aimed, and the discipline the law imposes on the process.

## Learning Objectives

Define the risk profile as the deck presents it, and distinguish it from the register.
Identify indicator types the deck teaches — trader behaviour, goods, origin, route, value patterns — as indicator categories, not thresholds.
Explain intelligence sources and the joint-control provision (s.3(3)) that lets them be shared with cooperating foreign customs administrations.
State the targeting rule the Act implies: controls remain "primarily" risk-based, with random checks still provided for (s.3(1)).

## Key Terms

Authority :: the Ghana Revenue Authority established under the Ghana Revenue Authority Act, 2009 (Act 791)

## Source anchors

- `ACT` s.3(1) — controls including random checks; s.3(3) — joint control and
  targeting activities with cooperating foreign customs administrations; s.3(4) —
  confidentiality of profiles and assessment data. Quote each.
- `CPC-RISK` — the deck's profiles/indicators/targeting sections; cite slides.
- TIMETABLE — the programme's targeting topics.

**Fact-check caution.** Never state a live targeting criterion, score or threshold —
those are operational and confidential (s.3(4)). Indicator categories come from the
deck's slides with slide numbers; everything else stays generic. Any named system
waits for the named-systems fact sheet.
