# LESSON BRIEF 12.1 — Risk-management foundations, mandate and objectives

## Summary

Ghana's customs law does not examine everything: it examines by risk. Section 3 makes
risk management the primary basis of customs control, with objectives the Act states —
identifying and evaluating risks, and developing counter-measures from international,
national and local data. The risk-management deck supplies the practice. This lesson
teaches the mandate and its objectives as the law writes them.

## Learning Objectives

State s.3's mandate: controls including random checks, conducted primarily on the basis of risk management.
Quote the provision's two objectives: identifying and evaluating risks, and developing counter-measures from international, national and local level data.
Explain why the law chose risk-based control over uniform examination, and what that means at a clearance.
Identify the deck's foundations content — what risk management is and what it is for — as the practice-side frame.

## Key Terms

proper officer :: an officer whose right or duty it is to exact the performance of or to perform the act referred to
