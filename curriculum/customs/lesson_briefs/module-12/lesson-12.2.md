# LESSON BRIEF 12.2 — Risk identification, analysis, evaluation and risk registers

## Summary

Risk management is a method, not a mood: identify what can go wrong, analyse how
likely and how harmful, evaluate what deserves action, and record the result in a
register. The Act's confidentiality rule (s.3(4)) tells the learner how these
artifacts are treated; the deck supplies the working method. This lesson teaches the
cycle a customs risk team actually runs.

## Learning Objectives

Walk the risk cycle — identification, analysis, evaluation, treatment — at the method level the deck teaches.
Describe a risk register's anatomy: what an entry carries and who acts on it.
Explain the data sources s.3(2)(b) names — international, national and local level data — and how each feeds the cycle.
State the confidentiality rule (s.3(4)) for risk registers, profiles and assessment data, and why it exists.

## Key Terms
