## Summary (from curriculum)
This lesson introduces the application of data analytics and emerging technologies to logistics decision-making. Learners will examine how operational data can reveal patterns, identify inefficiencies, forecast outcomes, and support planning. The lesson also explores artificial intelligence, machine learning, robotics, digital twins, and other developing technologies, while considering their practical benefits, limitations, implementation costs, and governance requirements.

## Learning Objectives (from curriculum)
Define logistics data analytics and explain its importance.



Identify common sources of logistics and supply chain data.



Distinguish between descriptive, diagnostic, predictive, and prescriptive analytics.



Explain how data analytics supports transportation, warehousing, and inventory decisions.



Describe potential applications of artificial intelligence and machine learning in logistics.



Identify examples of robotics and automated equipment used in logistics facilities.



Explain the basic concept of digital twins in supply chain operations.



Recognise challenges involving data quality, privacy, security, and implementation costs.



Evaluate potential benefits and limitations of emerging logistics technologies.



Recommend appropriate technologies for selected operational problems.

## Key Terms (from curriculum)
Data Analytics :: The process of examining data to identify patterns, generate insights, and support decisions.

Descriptive Analytics :: The analysis of historical data to explain what has happened.

Diagnostic Analytics :: The analysis of data to investigate why an event or outcome occurred.

Predictive Analytics :: The use of data and analytical models to estimate likely future outcomes.

Prescriptive Analytics :: The use of analytical methods to recommend actions under specified objectives and constraints.

Artificial Intelligence (AI) :: Computer-based techniques designed to perform tasks associated with capabilities such as learning, reasoning, perception, or decision-making.

Machine Learning :: A field of artificial intelligence in which computational models learn patterns from data to perform tasks or make predictions.

Business Intelligence (BI) :: Technologies and processes used to collect, analyse, and present business information for decision-making.

Data Visualisation :: The graphical presentation of information to support interpretation and analysis.

Digital Twin :: A digital representation of a physical asset, process, or system that is updated or connected using relevant data.

Warehouse Robotics :: The use of programmable machines to assist with warehouse handling, movement, or fulfilment activities.

Autonomous Mobile Robot (AMR) :: A mobile robot capable of navigating and performing tasks with a degree of autonomy.

Blockchain :: A distributed ledger technology that records transactions in a linked and tamper-evident structure.

Data Governance :: The policies, responsibilities, and controls used to manage data quality, security, availability, and appropriate use.
