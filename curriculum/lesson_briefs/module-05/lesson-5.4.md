## Summary (from curriculum)
This lesson examines the importance of shipment monitoring, tracking, and effective customer communication in freight forwarding operations. Learners will explore how freight forwarders obtain shipment updates, interpret transportation milestones, communicate expected arrival and delivery information, and respond to delays or disruptions. The lesson also emphasizes accurate recordkeeping, proactive communication, and professional customer service throughout the shipment lifecycle.

## Learning Objectives (from curriculum)
Define shipment tracking and explain its importance in freight forwarding.
Identify common methods and systems used to monitor cargo movement.
Describe major shipment milestones from booking to final delivery.
Explain the meaning and operational importance of ETD and ETA updates.
Identify the information customers commonly require during shipment transportation.
Describe effective methods of communicating shipment progress to customers.
Explain how freight forwarders should respond to delays, schedule changes, and shipment discrepancies.
Recognize the importance of accurate shipment records and timely status updates.
Demonstrate how to prepare a clear and professional shipment status report.

## Key Terms (from curriculum)
Shipment Tracking :: The process of monitoring and recording the location, movement, and progress of goods during transportation.
Shipment Visibility :: The ability to obtain relevant and timely information about the status and movement of a shipment.
Tracking Number :: A unique reference used to identify and monitor a shipment within a carrier's or logistics provider's system.
Shipment Milestone :: A significant event or stage in a shipment's journey, such as cargo collection, departure, arrival, or delivery.
Estimated Time of Departure (ETD) :: The anticipated date and time when a transport service is expected to depart.
Estimated Time of Arrival (ETA) :: The anticipated date and time when a transport service is expected to arrive.
Actual Time of Departure (ATD) :: The recorded date and time when a transport service actually departed.
Actual Time of Arrival (ATA) :: The recorded date and time when a transport service actually arrived.
Shipment Status Report :: A document or electronic communication summarising the current progress and relevant details of a shipment.
Exception Management :: The process of identifying, investigating, communicating, and resolving events that disrupt normal logistics operations.
Delivery Confirmation :: A notification or record confirming that a shipment has reached its intended delivery point or recipient.
Customer Communication :: The exchange of relevant information between a logistics service provider and its customers throughout the service process.
Proactive Communication :: Providing timely information about expected developments, changes, or potential problems before the customer needs to request it.
