## Summary (from curriculum)
This lesson explains how freight forwarders obtain transportation rates, prepare quotations, and arrange shipment bookings for customers. Learners will examine the information required to request freight rates, the factors influencing transportation charges, and the procedures involved in comparing service options and confirming bookings with carriers or other service providers.

## Learning Objectives (from curriculum)
Define freight quotations and shipment bookings.
Identify the information required to request freight rates from carriers or logistics service providers.
Explain the factors that influence freight charges and transportation costs.
Describe the basic components of a freight quotation.
Distinguish between freight rates, surcharges, and additional service charges.
Explain the procedures involved in requesting and confirming shipment bookings.
Identify important information contained in booking confirmations.
Compare basic transportation quotations based on price, transit time, service coverage, and conditions.
Recognise common booking errors and their possible operational consequences.

## Key Terms (from curriculum)
Freight Quotation :: A statement of proposed charges and conditions for transporting goods or providing related logistics services.
Freight Rate :: The price charged for transporting cargo, usually calculated according to an applicable pricing basis.
Shipment Booking :: An arrangement made with a carrier or logistics service provider to reserve transportation capacity for cargo.
Booking Confirmation :: A document or electronic notification confirming that a carrier or service provider has accepted a shipment booking.
Freight Surcharge :: An additional charge applied to a freight service to recover or account for specified costs or operating conditions.
Chargeable Weight :: The weight used to calculate transportation charges, determined according to the applicable freight rating rules.
Gross Weight :: The total weight of goods, including their packaging, but excluding the weight of the transport equipment unless otherwise specified.
Volumetric Weight :: A calculated weight based on the dimensions of cargo, used in certain freight pricing methods.
Transit Time :: The estimated or actual duration required to transport goods between specified locations.
Estimated Time of Departure (ETD) :: The anticipated date and time when a vessel, aircraft, or other transport service will depart.
Estimated Time of Arrival (ETA) :: The anticipated date and time when a vessel, aircraft, or other transport service will arrive.
Freight Validity Period :: The period during which a quoted freight rate or commercial offer remains valid under its stated conditions.
