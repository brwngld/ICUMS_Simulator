## Summary (from curriculum)
This lesson introduces cargo insurance and the principles of risk management in logistics operations. Learners will examine common transportation risks, insurance coverage, exclusions, claims procedures, and the allocation of risk between trading parties. The lesson also explains how Incoterms, contractual obligations, and insurance arrangements influence financial responsibility when cargo is lost, damaged, or delayed.

## Learning Objectives (from curriculum)
Define cargo insurance and explain its importance in logistics.



Identify common risks affecting goods during transportation and storage.



Distinguish between cargo insurance and carrier liability.



Describe common categories of cargo insurance coverage.



Explain the importance of insurance exclusions and policy conditions.



Identify how Incoterms allocate transportation risk between buyers and sellers.



Describe the basic procedures involved in reporting cargo loss or damage.



Identify documents commonly required when submitting insurance claims.



Explain how preventive measures and insurance work together to manage cargo risks.

## Key Terms (from curriculum)
Cargo Insurance :: Insurance designed to provide financial protection against specified loss of or damage to goods during insured transportation or related activities.

Insured :: The person or organisation covered under an insurance policy.

Insurer :: The company or organisation providing insurance coverage under an agreed policy.

Insurance Premium :: The amount paid for insurance coverage.

Sum Insured :: The monetary amount for which an insured interest is covered under the policy.

Insurance Exclusion :: A circumstance, cause, or category of loss that is not covered by an insurance policy.

Deductible :: The portion of an insured loss borne by the insured under the applicable policy.

All Risks Coverage :: Insurance covering fortuitous physical loss or damage except where excluded by the policy terms.

Named Perils Coverage :: Insurance covering losses caused by specifically identified risks or events.

Carrier Liability :: The legal or contractual responsibility of a carrier for specified cargo loss, damage, or delay.

Risk Transfer :: The point or process through which responsibility for specified risks passes from one party to another.

Insurance Claim :: A formal request for compensation under an insurance policy.

Survey Report :: A document recording the findings of an examination of cargo condition, loss, or damage.

Risk Mitigation :: Actions taken to reduce the likelihood or consequences of an identified risk.
