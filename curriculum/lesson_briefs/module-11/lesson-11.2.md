## Summary (from curriculum)
This lesson examines the handling and transportation requirements for dangerous goods and cargo requiring specialised care. Learners will explore dangerous goods classifications, hazard communication, packaging, labelling, documentation, and applicable transport regulations. The lesson also introduces refrigerated, oversized, fragile, and high-value cargo, highlighting the importance of qualified personnel, appropriate equipment, and compliance with relevant safety requirements.

## Learning Objectives (from curriculum)
Define dangerous goods and special cargo.



Identify the major hazard classes used in dangerous goods transportation.



Explain the purpose of dangerous goods classification, packaging, marking, and labelling.



Recognise the roles of applicable maritime, air, and land transport regulations.



Identify the documentation commonly required for dangerous goods shipments.



Describe basic handling considerations for temperature-sensitive, oversized, and fragile cargo.



Explain the importance of trained personnel and approved handling procedures.



Recognise the consequences of incorrect dangerous goods declarations.



Identify situations requiring specialist advice or authorisation before transportation.

## Key Terms (from curriculum)
Dangerous Goods :: Substances or articles presenting hazards during transportation and regulated under applicable transport safety requirements.

Hazard Class :: A category used to classify dangerous goods according to their primary transport hazards.

UN Number :: A four-digit identification number assigned to specified dangerous goods or groups of dangerous goods under the United Nations system.

Proper Shipping Name :: The designated transport name used to identify a dangerous substance or article under applicable dangerous goods regulations.

Safety Data Sheet (SDS) :: A document providing information about a chemical's hazards, safe handling, storage, and emergency measures.

Dangerous Goods Declaration :: A document or electronic declaration providing required information about dangerous goods offered for transportation.

IMDG Code :: The International Maritime Dangerous Goods Code governing the maritime transport of dangerous goods.

IATA Dangerous Goods Regulations (DGR) :: Industry regulations published by IATA for air transport of dangerous goods, based on applicable international requirements.

ICAO Technical Instructions :: International technical instructions governing the safe transport of dangerous goods by air.

Refrigerated Cargo :: Goods requiring controlled temperatures during transportation or storage.

Out-of-Gauge Cargo (OOG) :: Cargo whose dimensions exceed the standard internal or external limits of the intended transport equipment.

Fragile Cargo :: Goods requiring protection against damage from shock, vibration, pressure, or improper handling.

Hazard Label :: A label communicating the hazard classification of dangerous goods.

Segregation :: The separation of incompatible dangerous goods or cargo according to applicable safety requirements.
