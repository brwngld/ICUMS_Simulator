## Summary (from curriculum)
This lesson examines the procedures used to identify, investigate, and resolve operational problems during cargo transportation. Learners will explore shipment delays, damaged goods, missing cargo, quantity differences, and documentation discrepancies. The lesson emphasises incident reporting, evidence collection, stakeholder communication, corrective action, and the importance of maintaining accurate records when managing logistics exceptions.

## Learning Objectives (from curriculum)
Identify common causes of shipment delays, cargo damage, and discrepancies.



Explain how operational disruptions affect logistics performance and customer service.



Describe procedures for identifying and reporting shipment exceptions.



Explain the importance of documenting cargo condition and discrepancies.



Identify the evidence required to investigate cargo loss or damage.



Describe effective communication practices during shipment disruptions.



Distinguish between corrective and preventive actions.



Explain the role of carriers, freight forwarders, insurers, and customers in resolving shipment problems.



Develop a basic response plan for a delayed or damaged shipment.

## Key Terms (from curriculum)
Shipment Exception :: An event or condition causing a shipment to deviate from its expected transportation or delivery process.

Shipment Delay :: A situation in which cargo movement or delivery occurs later than the planned or agreed schedule.

Cargo Damage :: Physical deterioration or impairment affecting goods during handling, transportation, or storage.

Short Shipment :: A shipment containing fewer goods than the quantity specified in the relevant shipping or commercial records.

Over Shipment :: A shipment containing more goods than the quantity specified in the relevant shipping or commercial records.

Documentation Discrepancy :: An inconsistency or error identified within or between shipment documents.

Incident Report :: A record describing an unexpected event, its circumstances, and its observed consequences.

Exception Management :: The identification, investigation, communication, and resolution of disruptions affecting normal operations.

Root Cause Analysis :: A structured process for identifying the underlying causes of a problem.

Corrective Action :: A measure taken to eliminate the cause of an identified problem or nonconformity.

Preventive Action :: A measure taken to reduce the likelihood of a potential problem occurring.

Cargo Survey :: An examination of goods to assess their condition, quantity, damage, or other specified characteristics.

Claim Notification :: Formal communication informing a responsible party or insurer of a potential loss or damage claim.

Contingency Plan :: A prepared course of action for responding to disruptions or unexpected operational events.
