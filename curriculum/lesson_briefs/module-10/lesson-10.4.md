## Summary (from curriculum)
This lesson develops learners' ability to prepare professional logistics quotations and assess competing service offers. Learners will examine customer requirements, service scope, cost calculations, pricing margins, exclusions, quotation validity, and contractual conditions. The lesson also explains how to compare quotations based on total cost, reliability, transit time, service coverage, and commercial risk.

## Learning Objectives (from curriculum)
Define a logistics quotation and explain its commercial purpose.



Identify the information required to prepare an accurate quotation.



Distinguish between cost price, selling price, markup, and profit margin.



Calculate a proposed selling price using specified costs and pricing rules.



Identify the essential components of a professional freight quotation.



Explain the importance of clearly stating inclusions, exclusions, and validity periods.



Compare competing quotations using financial and operational criteria.



Recognise common pricing errors and hidden-cost risks.



Prepare a basic logistics quotation for a specified shipment.

## Key Terms (from curriculum)
Logistics Quotation :: A commercial offer stating proposed charges and conditions for specified logistics services.

Cost Price :: The amount incurred in obtaining or delivering a service before adding a selling markup.

Selling Price :: The amount charged to a customer for a product or service.

Markup :: The amount added to cost to determine a selling price, commonly expressed as a percentage of cost.

Profit Margin :: The proportion of selling revenue remaining after specified costs are deducted.

Service Scope :: The activities and responsibilities included in a proposed service.

Quotation Validity :: The period during which a commercial offer remains effective under its stated conditions.

Inclusions :: Services and charges expressly covered by a quotation.

Exclusions :: Services, charges, or responsibilities not covered by a quotation.

Rate Comparison :: The evaluation of competing prices and commercial conditions.

Service-Level Agreement (SLA) :: An agreement defining specified service expectations, performance measures, and responsibilities.

Commercial Terms :: The conditions governing pricing, payment, service delivery, and related obligations.

Quotation Evaluation :: The assessment of a commercial offer against cost, service, risk, and operational requirements.
