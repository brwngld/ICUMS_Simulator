## Summary (from curriculum)
This lesson introduces the methods used to calculate transportation expenses and determine the total landed cost of imported goods. Learners will examine freight pricing methods, chargeable weight, cargo volume, insurance, duties, taxes, and destination charges. The lesson also explains how Incoterms and commercial arrangements influence which expenses should be included in a particular party's landed-cost calculation.

## Learning Objectives (from curriculum)
Define freight costing and landed cost.



Identify the expenses commonly included in landed-cost calculations.



Explain how freight charges are calculated using weight, volume, or container-based rates.



Calculate chargeable weight using specified freight conversion factors.



Distinguish between customs value and total landed cost.



Explain how Incoterms influence responsibility for transportation and related expenses.



Calculate the landed cost of a shipment using supplied cost and tax information.



Identify how exchange-rate fluctuations affect import costs.



Explain how accurate costing supports pricing and purchasing decisions.

## Key Terms (from curriculum)
Freight Costing :: The process of determining the expenses associated with transporting goods.

Landed Cost :: The total cost of acquiring and bringing goods to a specified destination, including the expenses defined by the applicable costing policy.

Customs Value :: The value determined for customs purposes under the applicable customs valuation rules.

Cost, Insurance and Freight (CIF) :: An Incoterms rule under which the seller pays specified costs, freight, and insurance to the named destination port while risk transfers earlier at shipment.

Free on Board (FOB) :: An Incoterms rule under which the seller delivers goods on board the vessel at the named port of shipment.

Chargeable Weight :: The weight used to calculate freight charges under the applicable transport pricing rules.

Volumetric Weight :: A calculated weight based on shipment dimensions and an applicable conversion factor.

Freight Rate :: The price charged for transporting goods according to an agreed pricing basis.

Import Duty :: A customs duty imposed on imported goods under applicable tariff and customs laws.

Import Tax :: A tax imposed on imported goods under applicable national legislation.

Exchange Rate :: The rate at which one currency is exchanged for another.

Cost Breakdown :: An itemised presentation of the expenses contributing to a total cost.

Unit Landed Cost :: The landed cost allocated to one unit of an imported product.
