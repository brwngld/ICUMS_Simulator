## Summary (from curriculum)
This lesson examines additional logistics expenses that arise from cargo storage, container usage, shipment delays, and operational services. Learners will explore how demurrage, detention, terminal storage, handling, and other charges are calculated. Emphasis is placed on identifying chargeable periods, interpreting tariffs, reviewing invoices, and preventing avoidable costs through effective shipment coordination.

## Learning Objectives (from curriculum)
Identify common additional charges associated with international shipments.



Distinguish between demurrage, detention, and terminal storage charges.



Explain how free-time allowances affect container-related costs.



Interpret daily rates and progressive charging structures.



Calculate charges using specified tariffs and chargeable periods.



Identify circumstances that cause additional shipping and terminal expenses.



Explain how delays in documentation and cargo collection affect logistics costs.



Describe methods for reviewing and disputing incorrect charges.



Recommend operational measures to minimise avoidable expenses.

## Key Terms (from curriculum)
Demurrage :: A charge commonly applied when a container remains within a terminal or designated facility beyond the applicable free-time allowance.

Detention :: A charge commonly applied when a container is retained outside the terminal or designated facility beyond the permitted free-time allowance.

Storage Charge :: A fee for keeping cargo or containers at a facility under the applicable tariff.

Free Time :: A specified period during which particular container or storage charges are not applied.

Last Free Day :: The final day of an applicable free-time allowance.

Chargeable Period :: The period for which fees are calculated under an applicable tariff or agreement.

Progressive Tariff :: A charging structure in which daily rates increase over successive periods.

Terminal Handling Charge (THC) :: A charge associated with specified cargo or container handling services at a terminal.

Documentation Fee :: A fee charged for preparing, issuing, or processing specified documents.

Container Cleaning Fee :: A charge for cleaning a container when required under the applicable equipment-return conditions.

Container Repair Charge :: A charge for repairing container damage where responsibility is established under the applicable agreement.

Invoice Dispute :: A formal challenge concerning the accuracy or validity of a billed charge.

Cost Avoidance :: Measures taken to prevent unnecessary expenditure before it occurs.
