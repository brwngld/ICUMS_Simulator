## Summary (from curriculum)
This lesson examines the different expenses that contribute to the total cost of logistics operations. Learners will explore transportation, warehousing, inventory holding, cargo handling, administration, insurance, and distribution costs. The lesson also explains how direct and indirect costs influence operational decisions, pricing, profitability, and customer service.

## Learning Objectives (from curriculum)
Define total logistics cost and explain its importance in business operations.
Identify the major components of logistics expenditure.
Distinguish between direct and indirect logistics costs.
Explain the differences between fixed and variable costs.
Describe how transportation, storage, and inventory decisions affect total costs.
Identify opportunities to reduce logistics expenditure without compromising service quality.
Explain the relationship between logistics costs, profitability, and customer satisfaction.
Analyze a simple logistics operation to identify its major cost drivers.

## Key Terms (from curriculum)
Total Logistics Cost :: The combined expenditure associated with planning, transporting, handling, storing, managing, and distributing goods.

Transportation Cost :: The expense incurred in moving goods between locations.

Warehousing Cost :: The expense associated with operating storage facilities and handling goods within them.

Inventory Holding Cost :: The cost of maintaining inventory over time, including capital, storage, insurance, and obsolescence expenses.

Cargo Handling Cost :: The expense incurred in loading, unloading, lifting, moving, and securing cargo.

Direct Cost :: An expense that can be specifically attributed to a particular shipment, service, or activity.

Indirect Cost :: An expense shared across multiple activities that cannot be directly attributed to a single shipment without allocation.

Fixed Cost :: An expense that remains relatively unchanged within a relevant operating range over a specified period.

Variable Cost :: An expense that changes according to the level of activity or service provided.

Cost Driver :: A factor that influences the amount of expenditure incurred by a logistics activity.

Cost Allocation :: The process of assigning shared expenses to specific operations, customers, or services.

Operating Margin :: The proportion of revenue remaining after deducting operating expenses, calculated according to the applicable accounting method.
