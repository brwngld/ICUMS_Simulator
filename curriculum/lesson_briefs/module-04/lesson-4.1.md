## Summary (from curriculum)
This lesson introduces the three major forms of international goods movement: importing, exporting, and transit trade. Learners will understand how goods move between countries, the purposes of these trade activities, the responsibilities of participating businesses, and the basic regulatory and logistical requirements involved in cross-border transactions.

## Learning Objectives (from curriculum)
Define international trade and explain its importance to national economies and businesses.
Distinguish between importing, exporting, and transit trade.
Identify the major participants involved in international trade transactions.
Explain the basic processes involved in importing and exporting goods.
Describe the purpose and characteristics of transit trade.
Identify common documents and regulatory requirements associated with cross-border trade.
Explain the relationship between international trade and logistics operations.
Recognise common challenges associated with international goods movement.

## Key Terms (from curriculum)
International Trade :: The exchange of goods and services between individuals, businesses, or organisations located in different countries.
Import :: The bringing of goods into a country's customs territory from another country or territory, subject to applicable customs laws.
Export :: The sending of goods from one country or customs territory to another.
Transit Trade :: The movement of goods through a country or customs territory under applicable transit arrangements, generally without the goods being entered for domestic consumption.
Importer :: A person or organisation responsible for bringing goods into a country in accordance with applicable import requirements.
Exporter :: A person or organisation that sends or sells goods to a destination outside its country or customs territory.
Country of Origin :: The country in which goods are considered to have originated under applicable rules of origin.
Country of Destination :: The country to which goods are intended to be delivered.
Customs Territory :: A geographical area within which a particular set of customs laws applies.
Trade Barrier :: A government-imposed measure, such as a tariff, quota, or regulatory restriction, that affects international trade.
Customs Declaration :: An official statement submitted to customs providing information required for a particular customs procedure.
