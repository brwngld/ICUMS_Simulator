## Summary (from curriculum)
This lesson examines the commercial relationships between buyers and sellers in international trade. Learners will explore the responsibilities of trading parties, the importance of clearly defined contractual terms, and the role of international sales agreements in establishing obligations relating to goods, delivery, payment, risk, and dispute resolution.

## Learning Objectives (from curriculum)
Identify the roles and responsibilities of buyers and sellers in international trade.
Explain how international buying and selling transactions are established.
Describe the purpose of an international sales contract.
Identify essential terms commonly included in international trade agreements.
Explain the importance of clearly defining delivery, payment, and contractual obligations.
Distinguish between commercial sales contracts and government-level trade agreements.
Recognize common commercial risks arising from unclear contractual arrangements.
Explain how contractual agreements support effective logistics planning and shipment coordination.

## Key Terms (from curriculum)
Buyer :: A person or organization that purchases goods or services from another party.
Seller :: A person or organization that supplies goods or services in exchange for an agreed consideration.
International Sales Contract :: A legally binding agreement governing the sale of goods between parties involved in an international commercial transaction.
Purchase Order (PO) :: A document issued by a buyer specifying the goods or services requested and the proposed purchase terms.
Pro Forma Invoice :: A preliminary commercial document issued by a seller outlining proposed goods, prices, and transaction terms before a final sale or shipment.
Contractual Obligation :: A duty or responsibility that a party is legally required to fulfil under an agreement.
Terms of Sale :: The agreed commercial conditions governing a transaction, including matters such as price, delivery, and payment.
Trade Agreement :: An agreement between countries or trading blocs establishing rules or conditions governing trade between them.
Free Trade Agreement (FTA) :: An agreement between countries or trading blocs intended to reduce or eliminate specified barriers to trade.
Breach of Contract :: A failure by a party to fulfill a contractual obligation without lawful justification.
Dispute Resolution :: The process of resolving disagreements between parties through negotiation, mediation, arbitration, litigation, or another agreed mechanism.
