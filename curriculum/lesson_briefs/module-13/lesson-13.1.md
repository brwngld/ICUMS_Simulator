## Summary (from curriculum)
This lesson examines how organisations anticipate future customer demand and plan the resources required to meet it. Learners will explore forecasting methods, historical demand analysis, seasonal variations, supply planning, and the relationship between demand forecasts and procurement, inventory, production, and distribution decisions. The lesson also introduces forecast accuracy and the operational consequences of inaccurate predictions.

## Learning Objectives (from curriculum)
Define demand forecasting and supply planning.



Explain the importance of forecasting in logistics and supply chain management.



Identify qualitative and quantitative forecasting methods.



Describe how historical sales data and seasonal patterns influence forecasts.



Explain the relationship between demand forecasts and inventory requirements.



Identify factors affecting supply availability and replenishment planning.



Distinguish between forecast demand and actual demand.



Calculate simple demand forecasts using historical data.



Explain how forecast errors affect stock availability, transportation, and operating costs.



Recommend basic measures for improving supply planning accuracy.

## Key Terms (from curriculum)
Demand Forecasting :: The process of estimating future demand for goods or services using available information and forecasting methods.

Supply Planning :: The process of determining how resources, inventory, and supply activities will meet expected demand.

Historical Demand :: Recorded customer demand from previous periods used to support forecasting and planning.

Qualitative Forecasting :: Forecasting based on expert judgement, market knowledge, or other non-numerical information.

Quantitative Forecasting :: Forecasting based on numerical data and statistical or mathematical methods.

Moving Average :: A forecasting technique that calculates an average from a specified number of recent observations.

Seasonality :: A recurring pattern of demand changes associated with particular periods or events.

Forecast Accuracy :: A measure of how closely predicted demand matches actual demand.

Forecast Error :: The difference between forecast demand and actual demand.

Demand Variability :: Changes or fluctuations in customer demand over time.

Lead Time :: The period between initiating a supply or replenishment activity and receiving the required goods.

Supply Capacity :: The quantity of goods or services that a supply system can provide within a specified period.

Sales and Operations Planning (S&OP) :: A coordinated business planning process that aligns demand, supply, and financial objectives.

Demand Planning :: The activities involved in analysing and estimating customer demand to support operational decisions.
