## Summary (from curriculum)
This lesson introduces performance measurement as a tool for monitoring and improving logistics operations. Learners will examine key performance indicators used in transportation, warehousing, inventory management, cost control, and customer service. The lesson also explains how performance data is collected, interpreted, and used to identify inefficiencies, establish targets, and support continuous improvement.

## Learning Objectives (from curriculum)
Define logistics performance measurement and key performance indicators.



Explain why performance monitoring is important in logistics management.



Identify common transportation, warehousing, and inventory KPIs.



Describe how delivery reliability and order accuracy are measured.



Explain the relationship between operational KPIs and customer satisfaction.



Distinguish between performance targets and actual results.



Calculate selected logistics KPIs using supplied operational data.



Explain how dashboards and performance reports support management decisions.



Identify limitations of poorly defined or misleading performance indicators.



Recommend appropriate KPIs for a specified logistics operation.

## Key Terms (from curriculum)
Key Performance Indicator (KPI) :: A measurable indicator used to evaluate progress towards a defined operational or business objective.

Performance Measurement :: The systematic collection and analysis of information used to evaluate operational results.

On-Time Delivery (OTD) :: The proportion of deliveries completed within the specified delivery time requirements.

On-Time In-Full (OTIF) :: The proportion of orders delivered within the agreed time and with the complete required quantity.

Order Accuracy :: The degree to which fulfilled orders match customer requirements.

Inventory Accuracy :: The extent to which recorded inventory information matches verified physical stock.

Warehouse Productivity :: A measure of warehouse output relative to resources such as labour hours or equipment time.

Transport Utilisation :: The proportion of available transportation capacity used during operations.

Cost per Shipment :: The logistics cost allocated to an individual shipment under a defined costing method.

Order Cycle Time :: The time between receipt of an order and completion of the defined fulfilment process.

Benchmarking :: The comparison of performance against internal targets, historical results, or relevant external standards.

Performance Dashboard :: A visual display presenting selected performance indicators and operational information.

Continuous Improvement :: An ongoing approach to identifying and implementing changes that improve processes and performance.
