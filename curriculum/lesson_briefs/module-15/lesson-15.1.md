## Summary (from curriculum)
This lesson examines how organisations develop logistics strategies aligned with business objectives and customer requirements. Learners will explore strategic decisions involving transportation networks, outsourcing, service-provider selection, cost management, and service quality. The lesson also introduces structured supplier evaluation and explains how organisations balance operational capabilities, financial considerations, reliability, and long-term business needs.

## Learning Objectives (from curriculum)
Define logistics strategy and explain its relationship with organisational objectives.



Identify major strategic decisions affecting logistics operations.



Explain how customer requirements influence logistics strategy.



Distinguish between in-house logistics and outsourced logistics services.



Identify criteria used to evaluate logistics service providers.



Explain the importance of cost, reliability, capacity, compliance, and service quality.



Describe the basic stages of service-provider selection.



Explain how supplier performance can be monitored and reviewed.



Recognise the risks associated with excessive dependence on a single service provider.



Develop a basic service-provider evaluation framework.

## Key Terms (from curriculum)
Logistics Strategy :: A long-term approach to organising logistics resources and activities to support defined business objectives.

Strategic Planning :: The process of establishing long-term objectives and determining actions and resources required to achieve them.

Service-Provider Selection :: The process of evaluating and choosing external organisations to deliver specified services.

Logistics Outsourcing :: The use of external service providers to perform logistics functions.

Third-Party Logistics (3PL) :: The provision of outsourced logistics services by an external organisation.

Fourth-Party Logistics (4PL) :: A logistics management arrangement in which a provider coordinates or integrates multiple logistics resources and service providers on behalf of a customer.

Request for Quotation (RFQ) :: A formal request inviting suppliers to submit prices and commercial terms for specified requirements.

Request for Proposal (RFP) :: A formal request inviting suppliers to propose solutions, service approaches, and commercial arrangements.

Supplier Evaluation :: The systematic assessment of a supplier's capabilities, performance, costs, and suitability.

Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) :: The total cost associated with acquiring, using, managing, and maintaining a product or service over a defined period.

Supplier Diversification :: The use of multiple suppliers or service providers to reduce dependence and improve flexibility.

Strategic Alignment :: The consistency between operational decisions and broader organisational objectives.

Vendor Performance :: The measured effectiveness of a supplier or service provider in meeting agreed requirements.
