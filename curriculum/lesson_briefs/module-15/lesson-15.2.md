## Summary (from curriculum)
This lesson examines the commercial and operational principles involved in managing logistics contracts and service-level agreements. Learners will explore contractual obligations, performance standards, pricing provisions, liability, reporting, dispute resolution, and contract review. The lesson also explains how clear agreements help manage expectations, reduce commercial risks, and establish accountability between logistics customers and service providers.

## Learning Objectives (from curriculum)
Define contract management and service-level agreements.



Explain the purpose of contracts in logistics service relationships.



Identify essential provisions commonly included in logistics contracts.



Distinguish between contractual obligations and operational performance targets.



Explain how service-level agreements establish measurable expectations.



Identify common logistics service-level indicators.



Describe the importance of liability, insurance, and dispute-resolution provisions.



Explain how contract performance is monitored and reviewed.



Recognise the consequences of unclear or incomplete contractual terms.



Evaluate a basic logistics service agreement against specified operational requirements.

## Key Terms (from curriculum)
Contract Management :: The administration and monitoring of contractual obligations throughout the life of an agreement.

Service-Level Agreement (SLA) :: An agreement defining specified service expectations, performance measures, and responsibilities.

Contractual Obligation :: A duty or responsibility established under a legally binding agreement.

Scope of Services :: The activities and deliverables a service provider is required to perform.

Performance Standard :: A defined level of service quality or operational achievement.

Key Performance Indicator (KPI) :: A measurable indicator used to assess progress towards a specified objective.

Liability Clause :: A contractual provision addressing responsibility for specified losses, damages, or obligations.

Indemnity :: An undertaking by one party to compensate another for specified losses or liabilities under applicable conditions.

Force Majeure :: A contractual concept addressing certain extraordinary events beyond the parties' control that may affect performance, subject to the agreement and applicable law.

Termination Clause :: A contractual provision specifying circumstances and procedures for ending an agreement.

Contract Renewal :: The extension or continuation of a contractual relationship under agreed conditions.

Dispute Resolution :: The process of addressing contractual disagreements through agreed or legally available mechanisms.

Service Credit :: A contractual adjustment or credit provided when specified service commitments are not met.

Contract Compliance :: Adherence to the obligations and requirements established by an agreement.
