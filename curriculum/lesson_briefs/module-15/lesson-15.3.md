## Summary (from curriculum)
This lesson examines how organisations prepare for, respond to, and recover from disruptions affecting logistics and supply chain operations. Learners will explore business continuity planning, supply chain risk assessment, contingency arrangements, alternative suppliers, transportation disruptions, and recovery strategies. The lesson emphasises the importance of identifying critical activities and developing practical plans to maintain essential services during unexpected events.

## Learning Objectives (from curriculum)
Define business continuity and supply chain resilience.



Identify common disruptions affecting logistics and supply chain operations.



Explain the importance of identifying critical logistics activities.



Describe the purpose of business continuity plans.



Distinguish between risk prevention, contingency planning, and disaster recovery.



Identify strategies for reducing dependence on vulnerable suppliers or transportation routes.



Explain how alternative transport arrangements and inventory buffers support resilience.



Describe the importance of communication during operational disruptions.



Identify basic measures used to evaluate business continuity readiness.



Develop a simple contingency plan for a major logistics disruption.

## Key Terms (from curriculum)
Business Continuity :: The capability of an organisation to maintain or resume essential activities during and after a disruption.

Business Continuity Plan (BCP) :: A documented plan describing how an organisation will respond to disruptions and maintain or restore critical operations.

Supply Chain Resilience :: The ability of a supply chain to prepare for, respond to, adapt to, and recover from disruptions.

Operational Disruption :: An event that interrupts or significantly affects normal business activities.

Risk Assessment :: The process of identifying risks and evaluating their likelihood and potential consequences.

Business Impact Analysis (BIA) :: The assessment of how disruptions to business activities may affect organisational operations and objectives.

Contingency Planning :: The preparation of alternative actions for responding to unexpected events.

Disaster Recovery :: The restoration of systems, facilities, or operations following a major disruptive event.

Recovery Time Objective (RTO) :: The targeted maximum period within which a disrupted process or system should be restored.

Recovery Point Objective (RPO) :: The maximum acceptable amount of data loss measured in time before a disruption.

Supplier Diversification :: The use of multiple suppliers to reduce dependence on a single source.

Redundancy :: The availability of additional resources, systems, or capacity that can support operations when primary resources fail.

Single Point of Failure :: A component or dependency whose failure can cause a wider system or operation to stop functioning.

Resilience Testing :: The evaluation of an organisation's ability to respond to and recover from simulated or actual disruptions.
