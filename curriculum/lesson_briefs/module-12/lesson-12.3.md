## Summary (from curriculum)
This lesson examines the coordination required among transport providers, freight forwarders, customs brokers, shipping agents, regulatory authorities, and cargo owners during international logistics operations. Learners will explore stakeholder responsibilities, information exchange, documentation flow, operational scheduling, and the management of communication challenges. Emphasis is placed on maintaining clear responsibilities and ensuring timely decisions throughout cross-border cargo movements.

## Learning Objectives (from curriculum)
Identify the principal stakeholders involved in cross-border logistics.



Explain the responsibilities of carriers, agents, freight forwarders, and regulatory authorities.



Describe how logistics participants exchange operational information.



Explain the importance of clearly assigned responsibilities and communication channels.



Identify common coordination problems affecting international shipments.



Describe how documentation requirements influence stakeholder interactions.



Explain the importance of scheduling and milestone monitoring.



Recognise the role of digital systems in improving logistics coordination.



Describe methods for escalating and resolving operational problems.



Develop a basic stakeholder coordination plan for an international shipment.

## Key Terms (from curriculum)
Stakeholder Coordination :: The organisation of activities and communication among parties involved in a common operation.

Carrier :: A person or company undertaking responsibility for transporting goods under a contract of carriage.

Freight Forwarder :: A logistics service provider that organises and coordinates transportation and associated services on behalf of customers.

Shipping Agent :: A representative appointed to perform specified duties on behalf of a shipping line or vessel operator.

Customs Broker :: A person or company authorised, where required, to assist clients with customs declarations and related procedures.

Regulatory Authority :: An institution empowered to administer or enforce applicable legal or regulatory requirements.

Information Exchange :: The communication or transfer of relevant data and documents between participants.

Operational Milestone :: A significant event or stage used to monitor progress in a logistics process.

Responsibility Matrix :: A structured record identifying which participants are responsible or accountable for specified activities.

Escalation Procedure :: A defined process for referring unresolved or urgent issues to an appropriate decision-maker.

Service Coordination :: The arrangement and synchronisation of services provided by different organisations.

Electronic Data Interchange (EDI) :: The structured electronic exchange of business information between computer systems.

Stakeholder Communication Plan :: A document specifying communication responsibilities, channels, timing, and reporting expectations.
