## Summary (from curriculum)
This lesson develops learners' understanding of how shipments are planned and managed when multiple transportation modes, countries, and service providers are involved. Learners will examine multimodal transport arrangements, cargo transfers, transport documentation, shipment tracking, and operational risk management. The lesson also explains how coordinated planning helps control costs, maintain cargo integrity, and achieve reliable delivery across complex transportation networks.

## Learning Objectives (from curriculum)
Define multimodal transportation and explain its application in international logistics.



Distinguish between multimodal and intermodal transport arrangements.



Identify the stages involved in planning a cross-border multimodal shipment.



Explain how cargo transfers between transportation modes are coordinated.



Identify common documentation requirements for multimodal shipments.



Describe the role of multimodal transport operators and other service providers.



Explain how transport mode selection affects cost, transit time, and risk.



Identify operational challenges associated with multiple border crossings and cargo transfers.



Describe methods for monitoring multimodal shipment progress.



Develop a basic transportation plan for a shipment involving more than one mode of transport.

## Key Terms (from curriculum)
Multimodal Transport :: The transportation of goods using at least two different modes under a single multimodal transport contract.

Intermodal Transport :: The movement of goods using multiple transportation modes, typically in the same loading unit without handling the goods themselves during mode changes.

Multimodal Transport Operator (MTO) :: A party that contracts as principal to undertake multimodal transportation and assumes responsibility under the applicable contract.

Transport Leg :: An individual segment of a shipment's overall transportation journey.

Mode Transfer :: The movement of cargo or a transport unit from one transportation mode to another.

Transport Hub :: A location where transportation routes or services connect and cargo may be transferred or consolidated.

Through Transport :: A transportation arrangement covering the movement of goods across multiple stages or services.

Multimodal Transport Document :: A document evidencing a contract for transportation involving more than one mode of transport.

Route Planning :: The process of selecting transportation routes and services to meet specified operational requirements.

Transit Coordination :: The organisation of transportation activities and formalities during cargo movement through intermediate locations.

Cargo Handover :: The transfer of custody or responsibility for goods between designated parties.

Shipment Visibility :: The ability to obtain timely information about the movement and status of goods.

Delivery Reliability :: The extent to which transportation services meet agreed or expected delivery commitments.
