## Summary (from curriculum)
This lesson introduces the primary participants involved in the production, supply, purchase, and consumption of goods. Learners will explore the roles and responsibilities of manufacturers, suppliers, buyers, and consumers and understand how their activities are connected within the logistics ecosystem.

## Learning Objectives (from curriculum)
Define manufacturers, suppliers, buyers, and consumers.
Identify the responsibilities of each participant in the logistics ecosystem.
Explain how manufacturers and suppliers contribute to the availability of goods.
Distinguish between buyers and final consumers.
Describe the relationships between producers, suppliers, businesses, and customers.
Illustrate how goods move between different participants in a simple supply chain.

## Key Terms (from curriculum)
Manufacturer :: A business or organisation that transforms raw materials or components into finished or intermediate products.
Supplier :: An individual or organisation that provides goods, materials, or services to another party.
Buyer :: An individual or organisation that purchases goods or services for personal use, resale, or business operations.
Consumer :: The individual or organisation that ultimately uses or consumes a product or service.
Producer :: An individual or organisation involved in creating or producing goods or services.
Wholesaler :: A business that purchases goods in relatively large quantities and resells them, usually to retailers or other businesses.
Retailer :: A business that sells goods directly to final consumers.
End User :: The person or organisation that ultimately uses a product.
