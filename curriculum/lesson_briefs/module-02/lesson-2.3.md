## Summary (from curriculum)
This lesson introduces the major physical facilities and infrastructure used in logistics operations. Learners will explore the functions of ports, cargo terminals, warehouses, and inland depots, including how these facilities support cargo handling, temporary storage, consolidation, distribution, and the transfer of goods between different transportation modes.

## Learning Objectives (from curriculum)
Define ports, terminals, warehouses, and inland depots.
Explain the primary functions of ports in domestic and international trade.
Describe the role of cargo terminals in receiving, handling, and transferring goods.
Identify the main purposes of warehouses in logistics operations.
Explain how inland depots support cargo storage, handling, and transportation.
Distinguish between ports, terminals, warehouses, and inland container depots.
Describe how logistics facilities interact during the movement of imported and exported goods.

## Key Terms (from curriculum)
Port :: A designated location with facilities where vessels can berth and cargo or passengers can be loaded, unloaded, or transferred.
Cargo Terminal :: A facility where cargo is received, handled, stored temporarily, and transferred between transportation systems.
Warehouse :: A building or facility used for storing, protecting, and managing goods before distribution or use.
Inland Container Depot (ICD) :: An inland facility used for handling and storing containers and, where authorised, providing customs-related services.
Container Freight Station (CFS) :: A facility where less-than-container-load cargo and other shipments may be consolidated, deconsolidated, and handled.
Distribution Centre :: A logistics facility designed to receive, process, and distribute goods to customers or other destinations.
Cargo Handling :: The physical activities involved in loading, unloading, moving, and securing goods.
Storage Facility :: A designated location used to keep goods under appropriate conditions until they are required.
Transshipment :: The transfer of cargo from one vessel or transport service to another during its journey to the final destination.
