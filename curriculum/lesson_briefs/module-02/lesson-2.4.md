## Summary (from curriculum)
This lesson introduces the regulatory institutions and service providers responsible for supporting, supervising, and facilitating logistics operations. Learners will examine the roles of customs authorities, port authorities, transport regulators, inspection agencies, and private logistics companies, with emphasis on how regulation, compliance, and service coordination contribute to the safe and efficient movement of goods.

## Learning Objectives (from curriculum)
Define regulatory authorities and logistics service providers.
Identify the major categories of regulatory institutions involved in logistics operations.
Explain the role of customs authorities in controlling the movement of goods across borders.
Describe the responsibilities of port authorities and transportation regulators.
Recognise the functions of inspection and other specialised regulatory agencies.
Distinguish between regulatory authorities and commercial logistics service providers.
Explain why regulatory compliance and cooperation among stakeholders are important in logistics operations.

## Key Terms (from curriculum)
Regulatory Authority :: A government institution or legally authorised body responsible for establishing, administering, or enforcing rules within a particular sector.
Customs Authority :: A government agency responsible for administering customs laws and controlling goods entering, leaving, or transiting a customs territory.
Port Authority :: An organisation responsible for administering, regulating, or managing port facilities and activities within its mandate.
Transport Regulator :: A public authority responsible for overseeing compliance with laws and standards governing transportation activities.
Inspection Agency :: An organisation authorised to examine goods, facilities, documents, or processes to determine compliance with specified requirements.
Logistics Service Provider (LSP) :: A company that provides services such as transportation, warehousing, freight forwarding, distribution, or logistics coordination.
Customs Broker :: A person or company authorised, where required by national law, to assist clients with customs declarations and clearance procedures.
Regulatory Compliance :: Adherence to applicable laws, regulations, standards, and official requirements.
Cargo Inspection :: The examination of goods to verify their characteristics, condition, documentation, or compliance with applicable requirements.
