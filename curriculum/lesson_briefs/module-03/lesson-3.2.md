## Summary (from curriculum)
This lesson introduces the major transportation modes used in domestic and international logistics. Learners will examine sea, air, road, rail, and multimodal transportation, including their characteristics, advantages, limitations, and suitability for different types of cargo and delivery requirements.

## Learning Objectives (from curriculum)
Identify the major modes of transportation used in logistics operations.
Describe the characteristics of sea, air, road, and rail transportation.
Explain the advantages and limitations of each transportation mode.
Define multimodal transportation and explain how different modes can be combined.
Identify factors influencing the selection of a transportation mode.
Compare transportation modes based on cost, speed, capacity, accessibility, and cargo suitability.
Select an appropriate transportation mode for a basic logistics scenario.

## Key Terms (from curriculum)
Sea Freight :: The transportation of goods by vessels across seas, oceans, or other navigable waterways.
Air Freight :: The transportation of goods by aircraft.
Road Freight :: The transportation of goods using road vehicles such as trucks and vans.
Rail Freight :: The transportation of goods using trains operating on railway networks.
Multimodal Transport :: The transportation of goods using at least two different modes of transport under a single multimodal transport contract.
Intermodal Transport :: The movement of goods using two or more transportation modes, typically in the same loading unit, without handling the goods themselves when changing modes.
Transit Time :: The time required for goods to travel between specified points in a transportation journey.
Freight Capacity :: The quantity or weight of cargo that a transport vehicle or service can accommodate.
Transport Network :: A system of routes, facilities, and connections used to move goods or passengers between locations.
Last-Mile Delivery :: The final stage of transportation in which goods are delivered from a distribution point to the intended recipient.
