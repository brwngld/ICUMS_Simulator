## Summary (from curriculum)
This lesson introduces the basic documents used to identify, describe, transport, and deliver goods during logistics operations. Learners will explore the purposes of commercial invoices, packing lists, transport documents, delivery notes, and other supporting records, while understanding the importance of accurate documentation in shipment coordination and cargo movement.

## Learning Objectives (from curriculum)
Explain the purpose of documentation in logistics operations.
Identify common documents used during the movement of goods.
Describe the basic functions of commercial invoices and packing lists.
Recognise the purpose of bills of lading, air waybills, and road transport documents.
Explain the role of delivery notes and proof-of-delivery records.
Identify common shipment details recorded in logistics documents.
Explain why accurate and consistent documentation is important.
Recognize basic documentation errors that may cause shipment delays or discrepancies.

## Key Terms (from curriculum)
Logistics Documentation :: The documents and records used to support, control, and verify logistics activities.
Commercial Invoice :: A document issued by a seller that provides details of goods sold, their value, and the commercial transaction.
Packing List :: A document describing the contents, quantities, packaging, weights, or dimensions of goods in a shipment.
Bill of Lading (B/L) :: A maritime transport document issued by or on behalf of a carrier that acknowledges receipt of goods and provides evidence of the contract of carriage; certain forms also function as documents of title.
Air Waybill (AWB) :: A non-negotiable air transport document that provides evidence of the contract of carriage and acknowledges receipt of cargo for air transportation.
Consignment Note :: A transport document containing information about goods entrusted to a carrier for delivery.
Delivery Note :: A document accompanying goods that identifies the items and quantities being delivered.
Proof of Delivery (POD) :: A document or electronic confirmation showing that a shipment has been delivered or received.
Shipment Reference Number :: A unique identifier assigned to a shipment or logistics transaction for tracking and recordkeeping.
Document Discrepancy :: An inconsistency, omission, or error identified within a document or between related documents.
