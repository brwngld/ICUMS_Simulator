## Summary (from curriculum)
This lesson explains the stages involved in moving goods from their point of origin to the final buyer or customer. Learners will explore how goods are prepared, collected, transported, handled, stored, and delivered, as well as the responsibilities of different logistics participants throughout the journey.

## Learning Objectives (from curriculum)
Describe the typical stages involved in moving goods from a supplier to a buyer.
Identify the main participants involved in the movement of goods.
Explain how goods are prepared and collected for transportation.
Describe the role of transportation, storage, and cargo handling during shipment movement.
Explain the importance of shipment tracking and communication between logistics participants.
Identify common factors that may affect the timely delivery of goods.
Illustrate the movement of goods from origin to destination using a simple logistics scenario.

## Key Terms (from curriculum)
Origin :: The location where a shipment begins its transportation journey.
Destination :: The location where goods are intended to be delivered.
Consignor :: The person or organisation that sends goods to another party.
Consignee :: The person or organisation designated to receive goods.
Shipment :: A quantity of goods transported together under a particular transport arrangement.
Cargo Movement :: The physical transportation and handling of goods between locations.
Collection :: The process of picking up goods from a supplier, warehouse, or designated location for transportation.
Delivery :: The transfer of goods to the intended recipient or designated destination.
Shipment Tracking :: The process of monitoring the location and progress of goods during transportation.
Proof of Delivery (POD) :: A document or electronic record confirming that goods have been delivered or received.
