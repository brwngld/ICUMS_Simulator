## Summary (from curriculum)
This lesson introduces the different types of cargo transported through logistics networks and the methods used to package, protect, and handle them. Learners will examine general cargo, bulk cargo, containerised goods, perishable products, fragile goods, and dangerous goods, while understanding how cargo characteristics influence packaging, handling, storage, and transportation requirements.

## Learning Objectives (from curriculum)
Define cargo and identify common cargo classifications.
Distinguish between general cargo, bulk cargo, and containerised cargo.
Identify cargo categories requiring special handling or storage conditions.
Explain the purpose of packaging in protecting goods during transportation.
Describe common packaging materials and cargo-handling equipment.
Recognise the importance of cargo markings, labels, and handling instructions.
Explain how improper packaging and handling can result in cargo damage or loss.
Select suitable basic packaging and handling methods for different cargo types.

## Key Terms (from curriculum)
Cargo :: Goods transported by ship, aircraft, train, truck, or another means of transportation.
General Cargo :: Goods transported as individual packages, units, or consignments rather than as unpackaged bulk commodities.
Bulk Cargo :: Large quantities of unpackaged commodities transported directly in a vessel's hold, tank, or other suitable transport compartment.
Containerised Cargo :: Goods packed and transported inside standardised freight containers.
Breakbulk Cargo :: Cargo transported as separate packages or units rather than in containers or as unpackaged bulk.
Perishable Cargo :: Goods that may deteriorate or spoil unless transported and stored under suitable conditions.
Fragile Cargo :: Goods that are susceptible to damage from impact, vibration, pressure, or improper handling.
Dangerous Goods :: Substances or articles that present hazards during transportation and are subject to applicable safety regulations.
Packaging :: Materials and methods used to contain, protect, preserve, and prepare goods for handling, storage, and transportation.
Cargo Handling :: The activities involved in loading, unloading, lifting, moving, stacking, and securing goods.
Palletisation :: The arrangement and securing of goods on pallets to facilitate handling, storage, and transportation.
Cargo Marking :: The application of identification details, handling instructions, or other relevant information to cargo packages.
