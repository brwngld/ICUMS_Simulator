## Summary (from curriculum)
This lesson introduces the purpose, functions, and organisation of warehouses within logistics and supply chain operations. Learners will examine different types of warehouses, the activities performed within them, and the importance of effective warehouse layouts. The lesson also explores storage systems, warehouse zones, material handling equipment, and the principles of safety, accessibility, and efficient space utilisation.

## Learning Objectives (from curriculum)
Define warehousing and explain its importance in logistics operations.
Identify the primary functions performed within a warehouse.
Distinguish between common types of warehouses and their purposes.
Describe the major operational areas within a warehouse facility.
Explain how warehouse layout influences operational efficiency and cargo movement.
Identify common warehouse storage systems and material handling equipment.
Explain the importance of space utilisation, accessibility, and safe cargo storage.
Describe factors considered when designing or organising a warehouse layout.
Recognise common warehouse safety risks and basic preventive measures.

## Key Terms (from curriculum)
Warehousing :: The process of receiving, storing, protecting, managing, and preparing goods for distribution or use.
Warehouse :: A facility used for storing and managing goods before their onward movement, distribution, or consumption.
Distribution Centre :: A logistics facility primarily designed to receive, process, and distribute goods to customers or other destinations.
Warehouse Layout :: The physical arrangement of storage areas, equipment, workstations, and movement routes within a warehouse.
Receiving Area :: A designated warehouse location where incoming goods are unloaded, inspected, and recorded.
Storage Area :: A designated section of a warehouse where goods are kept until required for further processing or dispatch.
Picking Area :: A warehouse location where goods are selected to fulfil orders or other stock requests.
Dispatch Area :: A designated warehouse location where outgoing goods are checked, staged, and prepared for transportation.
Racking System :: A storage structure designed to organise and support goods, pallets, or other handling units.
Material Handling Equipment (MHE) :: Tools, machines, and vehicles used to move, lift, store, and handle goods.
Pallet :: A portable platform used to support goods during storage, handling, and transportation.
Space Utilisation :: The effective use of available warehouse space for storage and operational activities.
Warehouse Zoning :: The division of a warehouse into designated areas based on operational functions or storage requirements.
