## Summary (from curriculum)
This lesson examines the major operational activities involved in moving goods through a warehouse. Learners will explore the procedures for receiving incoming shipments, verifying quantities and conditions, assigning storage locations, selecting goods for orders, and preparing shipments for dispatch. The lesson also explains how proper coordination, accurate records, and standard operating procedures contribute to efficient warehouse operations.

## Learning Objectives (from curriculum)

## Key Terms (from curriculum)
Goods Receiving :: The process of accepting, inspecting, verifying, and recording goods arriving at a warehouse.
Goods Received Note (GRN) :: A document or electronic record confirming the receipt of specified goods into a warehouse or organisation.
Put-Away :: The process of moving received goods from the receiving area to their assigned storage locations.
Storage Location :: A designated position within a warehouse where goods are kept.
Order Picking :: The process of selecting goods from warehouse storage locations to fulfil an order.
Pick List :: A document or electronic instruction identifying the goods and quantities to be selected for an order.
Packing :: The process of preparing and protecting selected goods for storage, handling, or transportation.
Dispatch :: The process of releasing and sending goods from a warehouse to another location or recipient.
Dispatch Note :: A document or electronic record identifying goods released from a warehouse for delivery or onward transportation.
Stock Keeping Unit (SKU) :: A unique identifier used to distinguish and manage a particular product or inventory item.
Cross-Docking :: A logistics process in which incoming goods are transferred to outbound transportation with little or no intermediate storage.
Order Fulfilment :: The activities involved in processing, picking, packing, and delivering goods to satisfy customer orders.
Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) :: A documented set of instructions describing how a routine activity should be performed consistently and safely.
