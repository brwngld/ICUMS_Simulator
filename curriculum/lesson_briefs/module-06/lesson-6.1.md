## Summary (from curriculum)
This lesson introduces the commercial invoice and packing list as two essential documents used in international trade and logistics operations. Learners will examine their purposes, contents, and differences, including how they support shipment identification, cargo verification, customs procedures, and transportation planning. The lesson also explains the importance of ensuring consistency between commercial invoices, packing lists, and the actual goods being shipped.

## Learning Objectives (from curriculum)
Define commercial invoices and packing lists and explain their purposes.
Identify the essential information contained in a commercial invoice.
Describe the main components of a packing list.
Distinguish between the commercial and physical shipment information recorded in these documents.
Explain how commercial invoices and packing lists support logistics and customs procedures.
Identify common errors and omissions found in commercial invoices and packing lists.
Explain the importance of consistency between document details and the actual cargo.
Demonstrate how to review a basic commercial invoice and packing list for accuracy.

## Key Terms (from curriculum)
Commercial Invoice :: A document issued by a seller to a buyer that provides details of goods sold, their prices, and the terms of the commercial transaction.
Packing List :: A document describing the contents, quantities, packaging, weights, and dimensions of goods included in a shipment.
Invoice Number :: A unique reference assigned to an invoice for identification and recordkeeping.
Goods Description :: Information identifying the nature, type, and characteristics of goods being sold or transported.
Unit Price :: The price charged for one unit of a particular product.
Total Invoice Value :: The total monetary value of the goods or charges stated on a commercial invoice.
Currency of Transaction :: The currency in which the commercial transaction is priced or payable.
Gross Weight :: The total weight of goods, including their packaging, but excluding transport equipment unless otherwise specified.
Net Weight :: The weight of the goods themselves, excluding packaging and containers.
Package Count :: The total number of packages or handling units included in a shipment.
Shipping Marks :: Identification symbols, numbers, or other markings placed on packages to assist with shipment identification and handling.
