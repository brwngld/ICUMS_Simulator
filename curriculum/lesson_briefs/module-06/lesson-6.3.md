## Summary (from curriculum)
This lesson introduces certificates, permits, and other supporting documents commonly required in international trade and logistics operations. Learners will examine certificates of origin, import and export permits, inspection certificates, insurance documents, and other specialised records. The lesson explains how documentary requirements depend on the type of goods, country of origin, destination, and applicable regulatory requirements.

## Learning Objectives (from curriculum)
Define certificates of origin, permits, and supporting trade documents.
Explain the purpose of certificates of origin in international trade.
Distinguish between preferential and non-preferential origin documentation.
Identify common types of import and export permits.
Describe the functions of inspection, conformity, and insurance certificates.
Explain why different cargo categories may require specialized supporting documents.
Identify factors that determine documentation requirements for international shipments.
Recognize the responsibilities of importers, exporters, and logistics service providers in obtaining required documents.
Explain how missing or invalid supporting documents can affect shipment processing.

## Key Terms (from curriculum)
Certificate of Origin (COO) :: A document certifying or declaring the country of origin of goods according to applicable origin requirements.
Preferential Certificate of Origin :: A document or origin certification used to support a claim for preferential tariff treatment under an applicable trade agreement or arrangement.
Non-Preferential Certificate of Origin :: A document certifying the origin of goods for purposes other than preferential tariff treatment.
Import Permit :: An official authorisation required for importing specified goods under applicable national regulations.
Export Permit :: An official authorisation required for exporting specified goods under applicable national regulations.
Import Licence :: A government-issued authorisation allowing the importation of goods subject to licensing requirements.
Inspection Certificate :: A document confirming that specified goods or processes have been examined against stated requirements.
Certificate of Conformity (CoC) :: A document indicating that a product or consignment meets specified standards or regulatory requirements.
Certificate of Analysis (CoA) :: A document presenting the results of tests or analyses performed on a product or material.
Phytosanitary Certificate :: An official certificate issued by an authorised plant protection organisation concerning compliance with applicable phytosanitary import requirements.
Health Certificate :: A document issued or endorsed by an authorized body confirming specified health-related conditions or requirements for regulated goods.
Insurance Certificate :: A document providing evidence of insurance cover under the terms of an applicable insurance arrangement.
Supporting Document :: An additional document required to substantiate or complete a commercial, transportation, or regulatory process.
