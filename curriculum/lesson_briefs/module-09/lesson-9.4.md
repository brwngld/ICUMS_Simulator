## Summary (from curriculum)
This lesson examines the charges that may arise when cargo or shipping containers remain at ports, terminals, or other facilities beyond permitted periods. Learners will explore the differences between demurrage, detention, and storage charges, including how free time, chargeable periods, and tariff structures affect shipment costs. The lesson also introduces basic charge calculations and explains how timely documentation, cargo clearance, and transport coordination can help minimise avoidable expenses.

## Learning Objectives (from curriculum)
Define demurrage, detention, and storage charges.
Explain the purpose of free time in container shipping and terminal operations.
Distinguish between container demurrage, detention, and terminal storage charges.
Identify common situations that result in additional container or cargo charges.
Explain how shipping lines and terminal operators apply chargeable periods and tariffs.
Describe the factors influencing demurrage, detention, and storage costs.
Perform basic calculations using specified free-time allowances and daily rates.
Explain how delays in documentation, customs procedures, and cargo collection may increase logistics costs.
Identify practical measures for reducing avoidable demurrage, detention, and storage expenses.
Recognise the importance of reviewing applicable carrier and terminal tariff conditions before arranging cargo collection.

## Key Terms (from curriculum)
Demurrage :: A charge commonly applied when a shipping container remains within a terminal or other designated facility beyond the free-time allowance under the applicable carrier arrangement.
Detention :: A charge commonly applied when a shipping container is retained outside the terminal or designated facility beyond the permitted free-time allowance.
Storage Charge :: A charge imposed for keeping cargo or containers at a terminal, warehouse, or other storage facility beyond or within specified chargeable periods.
Free Time :: The period during which specified container or cargo usage or storage charges are not applied under the relevant tariff or agreement.
Free-Time Expiry :: The point at which an applicable free-time allowance ends and additional charges may begin to accrue.
Daily Rate :: A charge applied for each billable day or part of a day according to the applicable tariff.
Progressive Tariff :: A charging structure in which the applicable daily rate increases over successive periods.
Combined Demurrage and Detention :: A charging arrangement in which a shared free-time allowance or tariff applies to specified periods of container use inside and outside the terminal.
Container Return :: The delivery of an empty or otherwise specified container to a location designated by the equipment provider.
Empty Container Return Depot :: A designated facility where empty containers are returned after cargo unloading.
Last Free Day :: The final day of an applicable free-time allowance before additional charges become payable.
Chargeable Period :: The period for which charges are calculated under the applicable tariff or contractual arrangement.
Tariff Schedule :: A published or agreed schedule specifying charges, rates, conditions, and applicable periods.
Cargo Dwell Time :: The length of time cargo remains at a port, terminal, or logistics facility before departing.
Cost Mitigation :: Measures taken to reduce or prevent unnecessary expenses arising from logistics operations.
