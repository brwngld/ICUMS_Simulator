## Summary (from curriculum)
This lesson introduces the procedures involved in obtaining authorization to collect imported cargo from ports and terminals. Learners will examine the roles of shipping lines, freight forwarders, customs authorities, terminal operators, and transporters in the cargo release process. The lesson also explains the purpose of delivery orders, documentary requirements, applicable payments, customs release, terminal clearance, and gate-out procedures, while emphasizing the importance of completing the necessary requirements before cargo collection.

## Learning Objectives (from curriculum)
Define cargo release and explain its importance in import logistics operations.
Identify the major stakeholders involved in cargo release procedures.
Explain the purpose and function of a delivery order.
Describe the general procedures involved in obtaining cargo release authorization from shipping lines or their agents.
Distinguish between shipping line release, customs release, and terminal release.
Identify common documents and payment requirements associated with cargo collection.
Explain the role of customs authorities in authorizing the release of imported goods.
Describe the procedures involved in arranging cargo collection and terminal gate-out.
Recognize common problems that may delay cargo release.
Explain the importance of coordinating shipping lines, customs, terminals, and transport providers.

## Key Terms (from curriculum)
Cargo Release :: The authorisation and process through which cargo becomes available for collection or delivery after applicable requirements have been satisfied.
Delivery Order (DO) :: A document or electronic instruction authorising a designated party to take delivery of specified cargo, subject to applicable conditions.
Shipping Line Release :: The confirmation or authorisation from a shipping line or its agent that specified cargo may be delivered under the applicable transport arrangements.
Customs Release :: Authorisation granted by customs permitting goods to proceed under the relevant customs procedure, subject to applicable requirements.
Terminal Release :: Authorisation by a terminal operator to release cargo or a container after the terminal's applicable requirements have been satisfied.
Cargo Clearance :: The completion of required procedures and formalities for goods to be released or moved under an applicable customs or regulatory process.
Consignee :: The person or organisation designated to receive goods under a transport document.
Release Reference :: A document number, code, or electronic identifier used to confirm or process cargo release authorisation.
Terminal Handling Charge (THC) :: A charge associated with specified cargo or container handling activities at a terminal.
Gate Pass :: A document or electronic authorisation permitting specified cargo, containers, vehicles, or persons to pass through a controlled facility gate.
Equipment Interchange Receipt (EIR) :: A document or electronic record used to acknowledge the transfer of a container or transport equipment and record relevant condition details.
Cargo Collection :: The physical retrieval of goods from a port, terminal, warehouse, or other designated facility.
Gate-Out :: The recorded departure of cargo, containers, or transport equipment from a terminal or depot.
Cargo Hold :: A restriction preventing cargo release or movement until specified conditions or requirements have been satisfied.
Important distinction: Shipping line release, customs release, and terminal release are separate authorisations. Their order and requirements may vary depending on the country, port, terminal, cargo type, and applicable procedures.
