## Summary (from curriculum)
This lesson introduces the operational procedures involved in transporting goods by air. Learners will examine the roles of airlines, air cargo terminals, freight forwarders, and ground handling agents in preparing, accepting, handling, and transporting shipments. The lesson also explains cargo acceptance requirements, shipment booking, air waybill documentation, security screening, and the handling of cargo requiring special transportation conditions.

## Learning Objectives (from curriculum)
Define air cargo operations and explain their role in international logistics.
Identify the major participants involved in air freight transportation.
Describe the basic procedures for arranging an air cargo shipment.
Explain the requirements for cargo acceptance by airlines and handling agents.
Identify the purpose of air waybills and other air cargo documentation.
Describe common air cargo screening and security procedures.
Explain the importance of correct packaging, marking, and labelling for air shipments.
Identify cargo categories requiring special handling or acceptance procedures.
Describe how air cargo is handled from origin acceptance to destination delivery.
Compare the operational characteristics of air freight and ocean freight transportation.

## Key Terms (from curriculum)
Air Cargo :: Goods transported by aircraft for commercial or logistical purposes.
Air Freight :: The transportation of goods by aircraft.
Air Cargo Terminal :: A facility at or near an airport used for receiving, handling, storing, and processing air cargo.
Ground Handling Agent (GHA) :: A company providing ground-based services to airlines, including specified cargo handling activities.
Cargo Acceptance :: The process of examining and receiving cargo to determine whether it satisfies applicable transportation requirements.
Air Waybill (AWB) :: A non-negotiable air transport document that provides evidence of the contract of carriage and acknowledges receipt of cargo.
Master Air Waybill (MAWB) :: An air waybill issued by an airline or its authorised agent covering cargo accepted under the airline's contract of carriage.
House Air Waybill (HAWB) :: An air transport document issued by a freight forwarder to a shipper for an individual consignment.
Air Cargo Screening :: Security procedures used to examine cargo for prohibited articles or potential threats before transportation.
Unit Load Device (ULD) :: An aircraft pallet or container used to consolidate and secure cargo, baggage, or mail for air transportation.
Chargeable Weight :: The weight used to calculate air freight charges, determined according to applicable actual-weight and volumetric-weight rules.
Special Cargo :: Cargo requiring additional handling, documentation, packaging, or transportation precautions because of its characteristics.
Perishable Cargo :: Goods that may deteriorate unless transported and stored under suitable environmental conditions.
Dangerous Goods :: Substances or articles that present hazards during transportation and are subject to applicable safety regulations.
Cargo Manifest :: A document or electronic record listing cargo carried on a particular transport service.
