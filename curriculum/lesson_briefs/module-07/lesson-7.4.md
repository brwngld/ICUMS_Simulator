## Summary (from curriculum)
This lesson introduces the financial components associated with international cargo transportation. Learners will examine how freight rates are established, the differences between base freight charges and additional surcharges, and the factors influencing shipping costs. The lesson also explains common ocean and air freight charges, freight calculation methods, and the importance of reviewing quotations to identify the total cost of transportation.

## Learning Objectives (from curriculum)
Define freight rates, shipping charges, and surcharges.
Identify the major components of ocean and air freight pricing.
Explain the factors influencing international freight rates.
Distinguish between base freight charges and additional shipping surcharges.
Describe common charges associated with containerised and consolidated shipments.
Explain how weight, volume, container size, and transportation routes affect freight calculations.
Identify the differences between prepaid and collect freight arrangements.
Interpret the basic components of a freight quotation or shipping invoice.
Perform simple freight calculations using specified rates and shipment details.
Recognise the importance of verifying shipping charges before confirming transportation arrangements.

## Key Terms (from curriculum)
Freight Rate :: The price charged for transporting cargo according to an applicable pricing basis.
Base Freight :: The principal transportation charge before separately stated surcharges or additional services.
Freight Surcharge :: An additional charge applied to a freight service to account for specified costs or operating conditions.
Ocean Freight Charge (OFC) :: The charge for transporting cargo by sea under the applicable freight arrangement.
Air Freight Charge :: The charge for transporting cargo by aircraft, generally calculated using the applicable chargeable weight and tariff or agreed rate.
Bunker Adjustment Factor (BAF) :: A maritime freight adjustment or surcharge associated with changes in fuel costs, where applicable under the carrier's pricing structure.
Currency Adjustment Factor (CAF) :: A freight adjustment or surcharge associated with exchange-rate fluctuations, where applicable.
Terminal Handling Charge (THC) :: A charge associated with specified cargo or container handling activities at a terminal.
Documentation Fee :: A charge for preparing, issuing, or processing specified transportation or shipping documents.
Chargeable Weight :: The weight used to determine freight charges according to the applicable transportation pricing rules.
Volumetric Weight :: A calculated weight based on cargo dimensions and an applicable volume-to-weight conversion factor.
Weight or Measurement (W/M) :: A freight rating method in which charges are calculated using weight or volume, generally whichever produces the higher charge under the applicable tariff.
Freight Prepaid :: A freight payment designation indicating that the relevant freight charges are payable at or before shipment according to the agreed arrangement.
Freight Collect :: A freight payment designation indicating that the relevant freight charges are payable at destination or by the designated receiving party under the agreed arrangement.
All-In Freight Rate :: A quoted transportation rate that includes the charges expressly covered by the quotation, which may exclude other services or destination expenses.
Freight Invoice :: A document issued to request payment for transportation and associated services.
