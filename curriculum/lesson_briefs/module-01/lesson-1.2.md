## Summary (from curriculum)
This lesson examines the relationship between logistics and supply chain management, explaining their similarities, differences, and complementary roles. Learners will understand how logistics forms part of the broader supply chain and how both functions contribute to the efficient flow of products, information, and resources.

## Learning Objectives (from curriculum)
Define supply chain management and explain its purpose.
Distinguish between logistics and supply chain management.
Identify activities associated with logistics and broader supply chain management.
Explain how logistics supports supply chain performance.
Describe how coordination among supply chain participants improves operational efficiency.
