## Summary (from curriculum)
This lesson introduces the fundamental meaning of logistics, its purpose in business and everyday life, and the activities involved in moving and storing goods. Learners will understand how logistics connects suppliers, businesses, and customers while supporting efficient operations and timely delivery.

## Learning Objectives (from curriculum)
Define logistics and explain its fundamental purpose.
Identify the major activities involved in logistics operations.
Describe the scope of logistics in domestic and international trade.
Explain how logistics contributes to business efficiency and customer satisfaction.
Recognize the importance of coordinating goods, information, and resources.

## Key Terms (from curriculum)
Logistics :: The planning, implementation, and control of the movement and storage of goods, services, and related information.
Transportation :: The physical movement of goods or people between locations.
Warehousing :: The storage and management of goods in designated facilities.
Inventory :: The goods and materials held by an organisation for use, production, or sale.
Distribution :: The process of moving goods to customers or other designated destinations.
Logistics Operations :: The coordinated activities involved in transporting, storing, handling, and delivering goods.
