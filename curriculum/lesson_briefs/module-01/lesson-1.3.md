## Summary (from curriculum)
This lesson introduces the seven rights of logistics as guiding principles for effective logistics operations. Learners will explore how delivering the right product, in the right quantity and condition, to the right place and customer, at the right time and cost, contributes to operational efficiency and customer satisfaction.

## Learning Objectives (from curriculum)
Identify and explain the seven rights of logistics.
Describe the importance of each right in logistics operations.
Explain how the seven rights contribute to customer satisfaction.
Recognize operational problems caused by failing to meet logistics requirements.
Apply the seven rights to simple logistics scenarios.

## Key Terms (from curriculum)
Right Product :: Ensuring that the correct goods are supplied or delivered.
Right Quantity :: Ensuring that the required amount of goods is provided.
Right Condition :: Ensuring that goods arrive in an acceptable and usable state.
Right Place :: Delivering goods to the intended destination.
Right Time :: Delivering goods when they are required.
Right Customer :: Ensuring goods reach the intended recipient.
Right Cost :: Achieving delivery at an appropriate and economically sustainable cost.
