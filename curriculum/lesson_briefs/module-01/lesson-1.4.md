## Summary (from curriculum)
This lesson introduces the major types of logistics and explains their functions within business operations and supply chains. Learners will examine inbound logistics, outbound logistics, reverse logistics, and third-party logistics, including how each supports the movement, management, and delivery of goods.

## Learning Objectives (from curriculum)
Identify the four major logistics categories covered in this lesson.
Explain the purpose and activities of inbound logistics.
Describe how outbound logistics supports product distribution.
Explain the role of reverse logistics in returns and recovery operations.
Describe the services provided by third-party logistics companies.
Distinguish between the different logistics categories using practical examples.

## Key Terms (from curriculum)
Inbound Logistics :: The management of goods and materials moving into an organisation.
Outbound Logistics :: The movement and distribution of finished goods from an organisation to its customers or other destinations.
Reverse Logistics :: The movement of goods from customers or downstream locations back through the supply chain for returns, repair, reuse, recycling, or disposal.
Third-Party Logistics (3PL) :: The outsourcing of logistics activities to an external service provider.
Returns Management :: The process of handling goods returned by customers or business partners.
Logistics Outsourcing :: The use of external organisations to perform logistics functions.
