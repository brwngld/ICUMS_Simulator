Outcome guidance

Good outcomes describe what learners will be able to do after completing the course, not what topics are covered.

  • Start each outcome with an action verb (Explain, Identify, Apply, Describe…)
  • Be specific — tie the outcome to a task or decision a practitioner makes
  • Aim for 4–8 outcomes for a standard theory course
