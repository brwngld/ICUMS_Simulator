Know where you are

The simulator will use structurally familiar training screens while remaining visually distinct from the real ICUMS website.

Training actions have no official effect

Every transaction, document, status, payment, and release in this application is fictitious. Nothing is sent to customs, a shipping line, a terminal, or another external party.

Assistance changes by mode

Beginner practice includes guidance. Intermediate and Advanced practice progressively reduce assistance. Competency assessment requires independent work.
